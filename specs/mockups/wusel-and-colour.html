Wusel: seven poses

One drawing, seven parameter sets. Tufts are the family's member inks in board order; the body is brand of the shade. Sleepy is not sad. There is no sad pose.

Outfits

Free from the start, never earned, never lost. A member's choice shows wherever Wusel speaks to them; the Partyhut overrides it on their birthday.

Module bands

Modules are paper, like subjects and categories, so any hue is allowed. Text on a band is ink or ink-2, never ink-3. The member chips stay the loudest thing on the screen. At night there are no bands.

Decoration

For illustration only: blobs, confetti, the Rückblick's cards and the kid shadows. At most two per screen. Text on decoration is ink (light) or #14171A (dark).

The wall's header through the day

Computed on the device from the clock; cross-fades over 30 s. Text on the sky is ink. Member colours never sit on the sky. The Vorfreude strip shares the header slot with the Tischfrage and the Rückblick tile (Q57).

Kid register: sticker skin

Direction B's outlines and offset shadow, A's colours, kid register only. Press a card: it moves 3 px down on a spring. The last card shows today's flat style for comparison.
