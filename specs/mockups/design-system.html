WuselWall · design proposal · specs/17

Each person gets a colour. Everything else stays calm.

The palette is built so a seven-year-old, a parent with a coffee and a visitor reading the wall from 2.5 m all find their own things first. These five rules hold in every direction below; only the ground, shape and type change.

  • People are inkSaturated member colours for bands, dots, avatars and the active accent.
  • Things are paperSubjects, chores and shop categories get pale tints with one dark text colour.
  • Green means doneGreen is reserved for the brand and success, so no member can be green.
  • Never colour aloneEvery colour comes with an initial, an icon or a pattern. Try the vision switch.
  • AA in three themesEvery text pair is ≥ 4.5 : 1 in light, dark and the kitchen's night mode.

Chosen 2026-10-11

A — Küchentisch

A calm, warm kitchen table that the members' colours bring to life. Warm paper ground, Tanne green brand, hairline borders, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next throughout.

Strengths

  • Grown-up on the laptop, warm on the iPads
  • The member colours stay dominant
  • Most legible typeface for early readers

Weaknesses

  • The least exciting for a 7-year-old on its own

B — Pausenhof

A schoolyard sticker book. Sunny cream, Sonnengelb brand inside 2 px ink outlines, offset shadows, Fredoka headings over Nunito.

Strengths

  • Kids love it on sight
  • Outlines make every object read clearly at a distance

Weaknesses

  • Tiring on the adult laptop
  • Yellow on cream is 1.7 : 1, so the outline can never be dropped
  • Fredoka is less legible than Atkinson for early readers

C — Fjord

Nordic quiet. A cool fog ground, slate-blue brand, small radii, Figtree, lots of air.

Strengths

  • Most restful on the wall at night
  • The most "designed" look

Weaknesses

  • The slate brand sits close to the Ozean member
  • Feels like an office, not a kitchen
  • The pale ground weakens the member bands

Eight member colours, handed out in order

Each card shows the light ink with white text, the dark ink with dark text, and the tint. The first four are the most separable set of four for every kind of colour vision. Kakao keeps five apart; from the sixth member on, the initial carries identity, and the picker says so.
