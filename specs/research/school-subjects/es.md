# Spain: school-subject names and timetable abbreviations (LOMLOE era)

Research date 2026-10-23. Scope: Primaria, ESO, Bachillerato, co-official languages, timetable software, canonical grouping.

## 0. Evidence tags and limits

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[OFF]** | Official name from BOE / regional decree text that was fetched (URL in the source index). |
| **[OBS]** | Printed on a real school sheet that I downloaded and parsed (school + URL given). Verbatim. |
| **[SNIP]** | Reported by a search-result summary of a school PDF I could not open myself (Andalusian blogs, Aragon). Lower confidence. |
| **[CONV]** | Common convention from general knowledge. NOT verified by any fetched source in this research. Use only as a low-confidence alias. |

Honest limits:
- There is no official glossary of timetable abbreviations. Every abbreviation is a per-school (often per-software) choice.
- Searches for default abbreviation tables of Séneca, Delphos, Raíces, Itaca, aGora, Educamos, Alexia, Additio and GHC/Peñalara returned nothing citing one. Section 5 lists what is and is not known.
- `blogsaverroes.juntadeandalucia.es` timed out from this host, so Andalusian evidence is search snippets plus one Salesianos sheet (parsed).
- No real Basque, Navarrese or Balearic primary timetable was retrieved. Those regions are covered by official-name level and snippets only. The Catalan sheets are from Catalunya (Mestre Pla, Terra Alta).
- BOE texts: I read arts. 8-10/24/18 (RD 217), art. 8 (RD 157), arts. 9-14 (RD 243). The pages are huge and I did not reach the annexes with hours for RD 157/217.

## 1. Educación Primaria

Official frame [OFF, RD157 art. 8]: six areas in every course.
- **Conocimiento del Medio Natural, Social y Cultural** (may be split into *Ciencias de la Naturaleza* and *Ciencias Sociales*)
- **Educación Artística** (may be split into *Educación Plástica y Visual* and *Música y Danza*)
- **Educación Física**
- **Lengua Castellana y Literatura** (+ *Lengua Cooficial y Literatura* where it exists)
- **Lengua Extranjera**
- **Matemáticas**

Added: **Educación en Valores Cívicos y Éticos** in one or more courses of the third cycle (art. 8.2). **Religión** comes in via disposición adicional primera (no area name fixed in the text I read). Administrations may add a **segunda lengua extranjera**, another co-official language and/or a transversal area (art. 8.3), and lenguas de signos españolas (8.4). Weekly hours per cycle for Valores (0/0/50 h per cycle) come from a Eurydice summary relayed by search [SNIP].

## 2. ESO

Official frame [OFF, RD217 arts. 8-10, 18, 24]:
- Cursos 1.º-3.º: Biología y Geología; Educación Física; Educación Plástica, Visual y Audiovisual; Física y Química; Geografía e Historia; Lengua Castellana y Literatura (+ Lengua Cooficial y Literatura); Lengua Extranjera; Matemáticas; Música; Tecnología y Digitalización. Optativas must include Cultura Clásica, a digital-competence subject, and a segunda lengua extranjera if not already in art. 8.1.
- 4.º common: Educación Física; Geografía e Historia; Lengua Castellana y Literatura (+ cooficial); Lengua Extranjera; **Matemáticas A** or **Matemáticas B**. Plus three of: Biología y Geología; Digitalización; Economía y Emprendimiento; Expresión Artística; Física y Química; Formación y Orientación Personal y Profesional; Latín; Música; Segunda Lengua Extranjera; Tecnología.
- Educación en Valores Cívicos y Éticos in some course of the stage (art. 10). Enseñanzas de religión via disposición adicional primera (no name fixed). Atención Educativa not in the text I read.
- Programas de Diversificación Curricular (art. 24): ámbitos **lingüístico y social** and **científico-tecnológico**, optionally an **ámbito de carácter práctico**, plus at least three subjects outside the ámbitos. PMAR is the pre-LOMLOE predecessor [CONV; not in the LOMLOE text].
- Tutoría is not a subject; art. 18 regulates "Tutoría y orientación".

## 5. Timetable software and conventions

Established facts:
- **Period naming.** Sheets use numbered rows `1ª, 2ª, ...` (Galician [OBS GAL-MERCEDES]) or just time ranges (Valencia, Catalonia, Alpuente). The literal string "1ª hora" was not printed on the sheets I parsed.
- **Days.** Lunes-viernes, Dilluns-Divendres, Luns-Venres. Saturday/Sunday columns appear in a Salesianos export and are used (French on Saturday) [OBS SAL-ALCALA]. Basque weekday names [CONV].
- **Break labels.** `RECREO` (Spanish, Galician, Valencian), `ESBARJO` (Catalan), `PATI` (Catalan/Balearic), `ESPLAI` (Valencian long midday gap), `COMIDA` / `DINAR` (lunch), `ESMORZAR A L'AULA`; Basque `JOLASTORDUA` [CONV].
- **Jornada continua.** Murcia: lective activity 08:30-14:30 for continuous centres; non-continuous centres 08:30-17:00 with at least a two-hour gap and an afternoon of at least 1.5 h; minimum 25 hours/week distributed evenly Monday to Friday [OFF, CARM]. Madrid: 75 % of public centres have continuous hours; a draft decree favours split day with a continuous option in September and June [SNIP, press release]. Valencian CEIP: 22 h 30 min + 30 min esplai = 25 h [OBS RFORNOS]. Split day with a short Friday afternoon in Catalonia [OBS CAT-MPLA5]. September/June reduced timetable with 45-min sessions [OBS ALPUENTE].
- **Typical grids.** Primary: 09:00-14:00 with 30 min recreo at about 11:45 (Galicia) [OBS]; Catalan split day 09:00-12:30 + 15:00-16:30 [OBS]. Secondary: 55-min periods from 08:00 or 08:55 [OBS Valencia].
- **Cell content in generated sheets.**
  - Valencian IES PDFs: each cell is `CODE (room)`, for example `ANG (ANG1)`, `EF (PATI1)`; parallel optionals/supports are stacked in one cell; a footer table maps teacher to subject code ("Professorat / Matèria") [OBS VAL-2nA, VAL-1F]. The PDFs do not name the generator.
  - Catalan IES PDFs: cell shows `CODE`, teacher name, full subject name, room (for example `Cat / Úrsula Agut - Llengua Catalana 1 / C12`) [OBS CAT-TALTA].
  - Lugo PDF: teacher codes and names in cells plus a code-to-name table [OBS LUCUS].
  - Gallegan CEIP grid: full words, often wrapped over two lines (`COÑECEMENTO` / `DO MEDIO`), and "RECREORECREO" text artefacts after extraction [OBS GAL-MERCEDES].
- **GHC / Peñalara.** Publishers state it exchanges data with academic management systems and has an editor for import errors [SNIP educaciontrespuntocero.com]. A published GHC sheet from a Valencian school shows labels like `Matemat. 1`, `Fisi.Quími2` (truncated names) [SNIP DLFE-980731]. No default abbreviation table found.
- **Other tools.** Séneca, Delphos, Raíces, Itaca, aGora, Educamos, Additio, aSc: no documented abbreviation scheme found. Alexia Familias provides a family "agenda" with timetable, events, authorisations and canteen booking [SNIP]; the app works only if the school has enabled it.
- **Séneca-adjacent abbreviations on sheets.** 2019-20 IES (Cádiz) legend column "Abreviatura": `EF`, `ECDH`, `LCL`, `ING`, `REL`, `FR2`, `GeH`, `VE`, `ByG`, `FyQ`, `TEC`, `EPVA`, `MAC`, `MAP` [SNIP AND-GRAZ]. 2023-24 IES (Jaén) Bachillerato: `FIL`, `MAT I`, `ING I`, `TIC I` [SNIP AND-JAEN]. An Andalusian CEIP sheet spells everything out [SNIP]; the Salesianos 6º EP sheet also spells everything out, with the teacher name under each cell [OBS SAL-ALCALA]. BOJA 2 June 2023, Primaria weekly hours: CMNSC 2.5 h, EF 3 h, Religión/Atención educativa 1.5 h, Valores Cívicos y Éticos 1.5 h in 6º only, Educación Artística 2/1.5 h (odd/even courses) [SNIP, unverified].
- **Support teacher codes.** `PT` and `AL` normally appear on teacher timetables [CONV]. Valencian ESO sheets print `PIAR`, `REF xxx`, `PEILL`, `PT CLL` as parallel groups in the same cell [OBS].
