# WuselWall dictionary — calendar event pictures (specs/29).
#
# One block per icon key (a picture in app/assets/emoji/events/). Events have no shopping
# category, so there is no arrow. The format is described in shopping.txt: a word line is a
# language tag and comma-separated words in their natural spelling (the generator folds case,
# umlauts and accents). A word may appear under one key only.
#
# `star` is the fallback picture for every title that matches nothing; it has no words.
# Appointments with a person have a picture of their own (Zahnarzt, Arzt, Friseur, Tierarzt,
# the cleaner who comes). The bare word Termin stays out on purpose: no picture fits "an
# appointment" as such, so that title stays unmatched and shows the fallback.
#
# Where two pictures could claim a word the more specific one has it: a person or service
# (`cleaner`) apart from the activity (`broom`); the instrument or band practice (`guitar`)
# apart from choir, orchestra, concerts and general music lessons (`music`); racket sports
# (`tennis`) and riding apart from the team sports (`football`); cycling (`bicycle`) and
# skiing (`ski`) apart from hiking, but a holiday stays `plane` (Skiferien). `shopping` is the
# outing and the weekly shop alike; Dienst (a presence kind in the app) is not a word here.
