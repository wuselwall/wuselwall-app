Three new concepts drawn from the app's own palette (the four member colours, Tanne green and warm paper), compared with D, the icon already in the repo. Each is shown where it has to work: as an app icon at real size, in dark mode, as a single-colour themed icon, and next to other apps on a home screen.

AFamilien-W

Recommended as logo & app icon

The W is drawn with four strokes, one per family member, in their colours. Where two strokes meet they overprint, so the joints are where the family overlaps. It is the same idea as the dots on the week board: everyone has a colour, and together they make one thing.

Strengths

  • Reads as a W at every size
  • Explains the member-colour system at a glance
  • Calm enough for the work laptop

Weaknesses

  • Less playful for the kids on its own
  • A family of 3 or 5 still sees 4 strokes

BWuselchen

Recommended as mascot

The name, taken literally: a round little Wusel in Tanne green with a "w" for a mouth and four tufts of hair in the family's colours. The 7-year-old will name it within a day.

Strengths

  • Strongest appeal for the children
  • Can carry emotion: cheering when tasks are done, sleepy on the night display
  • Memorable among dozens of icons

Weaknesses

  • Reads as a kids' app on a parent's work laptop
  • The face needs care at 24 px; the tufts disappear first
  • A character means more drawing: poses, states

CWuselpunkte

A W made of nine dots, like magnets on the fridge or the "isst mit" dots on the board. Tanne green sits at the centre peak, and two small dots fly off to the top right: the bustle.

Strengths

  • Lively and light; "Wusel" without a character
  • Ties directly to the board's dot language

Weaknesses

  • Breaks up below 40 px: at 24 px it is confetti, not a W
  • Dotted letter marks are common; less ownable
  • No overlap story; colours repeat at random

DVier Figuren

Already in the repo · ADR-012

The icon added with the rename, docs/brand/wuselwall-icon.svg: four figures of different heights (two adults, two children) bustling above a strip of seven days, on a violet tile.

Strengths

  • Literally a family, and literally a week
  • Warm and immediately friendly
  • Distinctive tile colour on a home screen

Weaknesses

  • Off-palette: the violet tile, pink and cyan aren't the docs/17 values, and the fourth figure is white instead of a member colour
  • The seven-day strip disappears below 60 px
  • The tile is Lotta's violet, so the brand looks like one member's
  • No W, so it doesn't spell the name

Recommendation

Familien-W as the logo, Wuselchen as the mascot

They share the same four colours, so they read as one family. The W is the app icon, favicon and splash screen; it holds up at 16 px and on the work laptop. The Wuselchen lives inside the app, on the children's screens and on the wall, where it can react to things. That gives the kids the character without putting a cartoon on a parent's iPhone home screen.

Wordmark set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next ExtraBold, the app's own typeface: "Wusel" in ink, "Wall" in Tanne. Colours come from docs/17. Once a concept is chosen, the final mark becomes flat SVG files with the text converted to outlines, plus the iOS and Android icon sets generated from it.
