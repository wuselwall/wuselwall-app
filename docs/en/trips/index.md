# Trips

Trips are one place for everything you collect for a holiday: places you would like to go, weeks that suit everyone, flights, accommodation, the rental car, the plan for each day and the packing list. WuselWall does not search for flights or hotels and does not book anything. You search where you always search and bring in what you found. What WuselWall adds is something no travel site knows: when your family is free.

<Shot name="trips-list" alt="The list of trips with the entries Time windows and Where to?, a decided trip to Sardinia, a planned ski week and two ideas" />

## Where to find trips {#find}

- **Calendar**: on a tablet or computer a small button with a suitcase sits above **New event**. It opens **Trips**.
- **New event**: on a phone tap **New event**, then the tile **Plan a trip** ("Compare, book, pack"). It sits next to the template **Holiday / trip**, which still only creates one appointment.
- **In the calendar**: a planned or decided trip appears as one appointment over all its days. If you open it, a note says it belongs to a trip and is changed there. **Open trip** takes you there.
- **Today**: a decided trip shows a card there (see [During the trip](#during)).

If the entry is missing, your family's server is still older. It appears as soon as the server is updated.

## Who can do what {#who}

**Adults plan, children look forward.**

| Who | What |
|---|---|
| Adults | Create trips, collect options, compare, plan, pack. Every adult in the family sees a trip |
| Parents | Also: switch on [Weather and maps](/en/settings/services) and set the home place |
| Children | See the trip's appointment in the calendar and its countdown, such as "9 more sleeps". They have no Trips page |
| The wall display | Shows the appointment and the countdown. It changes nothing |

Everything in a trip is visible on every device of the family. A booking reference is therefore no secret from the family. A key for a weather service, on the other hand, stays invisible to every device.

## Ideas {#ideas}

Every trip starts as an **idea**: a place you would like to go, with no dates yet. Tap **New idea** at the bottom. Only the **Name of the trip** is needed, for example "Sardinia in summer". A picture, a **Destination**, a note and **Who is travelling?** are optional (nobody selected: the whole family). WuselWall takes the **Currency** from your last trip, otherwise CHF in Switzerland and Liechtenstein and EUR elsewhere; it is three letters.

Ideas stay. They are your wish list, even if you go somewhere else this year. You find ideas in the list under **Ideas**, trips under way or coming up under **Under way and coming up**, and past ones folded under **Past trips**.

## Time windows {#windows}

**Time windows** answers "When can all of us get away?". WuselWall works out the list for the next 18 months, you do not:

- Those who travel count (**Who travels?**; nobody selected means everyone).
- A day is **free** for a child when there is no school: at the weekend, on a public holiday, on a day the school is closed and during the school's holidays. A child with no school is free every day.
- A **time window** is a run of days on which everyone who travels is free. You choose the **Shortest stay**: 3, 4, 7, 10 or 14 days. School holidays are extended over the weekends and public holidays next to them. If no school child travels, the windows are the **long weekends**.
- Each window shows its days, the name of the holidays and the **leave days for the adults**: the weekdays in the window that are not public holidays.
- Below that it says what is **in the way**: appointments and presence entries of the people travelling, with name and day, for example "Business trip to Berlin". A window with a conflict stays in the list. You decide, not WuselWall. With no conflict it says **Nothing in the way**.
- If the children's holidays differ, the window covers the days they all share. Whoever has more days free is named beside it: "Paul is free from 25 Dec already".

<Shot name="trip-windows" alt="Time windows with the choice of shortest stay and of who travels, and a long weekend that needs one leave day" />

Each window has up to three buttons: **Take these dates** (for an idea you opened from its own page), **Where is it nice then?** (leads to [Where to?](#discover)) and **New trip in this window**.

The school holidays must have been imported, otherwise WuselWall only finds weekends and public holidays. How that works is described under [Timetable](/en/school/) in "Public holidays and school holidays"; you set the region under [Settings → Family](/en/settings/household#the-areas). Without a time window you can always enter the dates by hand: **Enter dates**.

## Collecting options {#options}

Open a trip. At the top it shows how far along it is, and below it has three tabs: **Plan**, **Pack** and **Prepare**. On the **Plan** tab you collect **options**, each with **Add option**:

- **Transport**: **Flight**, **Train**, **Car**, **Ferry**, **Bus** or **Other**, with **From** and **To**
- **Accommodation**: a flat, a hotel, a campsite, with **Address** and **Phone**
- **Rental car**: with a pick-up place and a return place
- **Activity**: the boat tour, the museum, a restaurant
- **Note**: for example "Paul's passport expires in June"

A **Name** and a **Price** are enough. Everything else can follow under **More details**: a **Link** (**Paste link** takes the address from the clipboard), a line of **Details** ("1 stop, baggage included"), **Start** and **End**, **Free cancellation until**, a **Booking reference** and a **Note**.

- **The price has a date.** WuselWall sets it itself whenever you enter or change the price, and shows it: "CHF 840 · Price from 12 Nov". Flight and hotel prices move, and you compare over weeks. Prices are estimates for comparing; WuselWall does not keep track of expenses.
- **Times are written as on the ticket**, in the local time of the place. If the flight lands in Olbia at 8:55, you enter 8:55. Nothing is converted.
- **Copying:** **Copy as another option** turns one option into a second, for example "the same flight a day later". The copy has no booking reference.
- Each option has a **State**: **Option** (found), **Chosen** (we take this one) and **Booked** (with a booking reference).
- A link, a phone number and an address open in the phone's own apps: the browser, the phone app and the maps app (**Open link**, **Call**, **Show on map**). WuselWall itself does not fetch them.

## Comparing and trying combinations {#compare}

Next to each group of options there is **Compare**. The page shows the options of one kind side by side, each as a card with the same rows in the same order: price (for accommodation also **per night**, for a rental car **per day**), from and to, start and end, details, **Free cancellation until** and the day of the price. The **Cheapest** carries a label. When free cancellation has passed, it says **Free cancellation has passed**. On a phone you swipe sideways; on a wide screen the cards stand side by side.

<Shot name="trip-compare-wide" alt="Comparing on a wide screen: three accommodation options and two rental cars side by side, with the bar showing the total and Fits together underneath" wide />

### Ticking {#ticks}

Every option has a tick. What you tick is the **combination you are trying out right now**. To begin with, the chosen and booked options are ticked. A bar at the bottom shows what the selection adds up to: the **total**, the days from the first start to the last end and anything that does not fit together. Nothing is saved while you try things out. Ticking and unticking costs nothing.

### What does not fit {#warnings}

If everything fits, it says **Fits together**. Otherwise it says how many things do not fit, and **Show all** lists them. These are all the possible ones:

- **A night without accommodation.** The campsite ends on 4 December, the return flight leaves on the 6th: "2 nights without accommodation", with the days.
- **The rental car starts before the arrival.** The car is picked up at 9:30, the flight only lands at 11:40. Or the other way round: it **ends after the departure**.
- **A rental car without any transport.** You ticked a rental car but no flight and no train.
- **Two accommodations overlap.** The hotel and the holiday flat are both ticked for the same nights.
- **An option without a price.** Then the total is a **minimum**: "At least CHF 4,440".

### Keeping combinations {#combinations}

**Keep as combination** saves the ticks under a name, such as "Cheap" or "Direct flight and beach hotel". Kept combinations stand side by side at the top of the page, with the total, the days, the transport, the accommodation, the rental car and the warnings. The cheapest complete combination carries the label **Cheapest**. You can **Rename** a combination, **Load into the ticks** (to change something and keep it again) and **Delete combination**. If you delete an option, it also leaves the combinations; the combination stays.

<Shot name="trip-combinations" alt="Kept combinations Cheap and Direct flight and beach hotel with total, days, transport, accommodation, rental car and a note that one thing does not fit" />

### Take this one {#take}

**Take this one** turns a combination into your plan, after asking you:

- Its options become **Chosen**.
- Whatever was chosen before (transport, accommodation, rental car) goes back to an **Option**.
- **Booked** entries stay as they are. WuselWall never changes anything you booked.
- With **Also set the trip's dates to …** the trip takes over the combination's days.

## From idea to decided {#from-idea-to-fixed}

A trip has three stages. The stage shows as a label on the trip.

| Stage | Meaning | In the calendar |
|---|---|---|
| **Idea** | A wish, with no dates yet | nothing |
| **Planned** | You pencilled in dates (**Pencil in these dates**) and are collecting options | a dashed appointment with "?" over all the days of the trip |
| **Decided** | Settled: **We’re doing this** | the same appointment, confirmed, with a countdown |

A decided trip counts down on the **wall** and on the **children's tablet**, like any appointment marked **We are looking forward to it** (see [Looking forward](/en/guide/rituals#countdowns)). You change the calendar entry **in the trip**, not in the calendar: its title, days and people are the trip's. If you open the entry in the calendar, **Open trip** takes you to the trip.

You can go back, too: **Back to planned** takes the countdown away. **Back to idea** (in the **More** menu) removes the appointment from the calendar; the trip and everything in it stay. **Delete trip** removes everything, including the options, the plan and the lists.

## The plan by day {#plan}

Once the trip has dates, the **Plan** tab shows each day as a card: **Day 1 · Fri, 27 Nov** In it are the chosen and booked entries in time order. Accommodation appears on the first day (**Check-in**) and on the last (**Check-out**), in between as a line **Night**; a rental car on the day of **Pick-up** and of **Return**. Entries without a date appear under **Without a date**. Above them a card shows the sum: **Chosen and booked**.

## Packing and preparing {#packing}

The **Pack** tab holds the packing list, **for everyone** and for each person on the trip separately. Tap **Add an item** to write something down. Two helpers save typing:

- **Suggestions** shows a starter list in groups: Documents, Health, Electronics, Clothes, Beach and sun, For the children. You choose what to add with **Add to list**. What each person needs is added for everyone who travels.
- **Like last time** takes the list of another trip, all unticked.

<Shot name="trip-packing" alt="The Pack tab with the progress 3 of 8, the buttons Suggestions and Like last time and the list For everyone" />

The **Prepare** tab holds the to-dos before the trip: "Check passports", "Cat: ask the neighbours". With **Days before the trip** you say when it has to be done at the latest; once the trip has dates, a date appears: **By 20 Nov**. Any adult can tick things off. There are no points and no ranking: this is not a chore, it is a reminder list.

## During the trip {#during}

On **Today**, the card of a decided trip appears 30 days before the start:

- **Before**: the days until you leave, what is **Still to do** (the to-dos that are due) and the packing progress, for example "3 of 8 packed".
- **During**: **Day 3 of 10**, what is on today in time order, **Tonight** with the accommodation including its address and phone number, and **Tomorrow** with the first entry. With the icons beside it you call or open the address in the maps app.
- A trip that is only planned shows a single line with its days and **All trips**.

A tap on the card opens the trip.

<Shot name="today-trip" alt="Today with the card of the decided trip to Sardinia: in 12 days, the open to-dos and the packing progress" />

**Without a network** you can read: the trips, their entries and lists stay on the phone as you last saw them, on a plane and abroad without roaming. Changing, ticking off and new entries need a connection to the server.

If there is a weather service (see [Weather and maps](/en/settings/services)), the trip shows the **Forecast** for the destination from seven days before the start until the end; before that, what is **typical at this time of year**, along with the drive from home by car and a map picture if a service can do that. What a service does not provide is simply missing.

## A booking's calendar file {#booking-file}

Railways, airlines and booking sites often attach a **calendar file** to the confirmation (it ends in **.ics**). If you open it in WuselWall (see [Adding an appointment somebody sent you](/en/calendar/#receive-event)) and you have a planned or decided trip, it offers **Add to trip**. With several trips, WuselWall asks **Which trip?**, shows what will be added and you tap **Add**.

- Every event in the file becomes an entry of the trip, as **Booked**. WuselWall guesses the kind from the words: flight, train, hotel, rental car. Title, day and time stay as they are in the file.
- If you open **the same file** again, for example after a rebooking, the entries are **updated**, not doubled.
- Cancelled events and events without a start are skipped. The preview says so.

WuselWall does not read PDF tickets and does not access your mailbox. A PDF stays where it is; the entry holds the booking reference and the link to it.

## Where to? {#discover}

**Where to?** answers "Where is it warm and dry then, and how far is it?" for a time away that you choose (to begin with, the next school holidays) with **Change**.

<Shot name="trip-discover" alt="Where to? with the time away and places such as Davos, Lake Garda and Tuscany, with distance, temperatures, rain days and hours of sun" />

- **The places**: a list of about 150 well-known destinations that comes with the server, mostly Europe and the Mediterranean, plus your own ideas that have a place. They are regions and islands such as Mallorca or Ticino, not single hotels.
- **Wishes**: day temperature at least or at most, **Little rain**, **Sea at least**, the **Kind of place** (Sea, Island, Mountains, Lake, City, Countryside, The North, Far away) and **Getting there, at most**.
- **Distance and effort** are estimates from the straight line from your **home**: **close by** up to 300 km, **a long drive** up to 800 km, then **a short flight** (up to 1,500 km), **a medium flight** (up to 4,000 km) and **far away**. WuselWall names no connection. For this a parent sets the home place (see [Weather and maps](/en/settings/services#home)).
- **The climate** (day and night temperature, rain days, sun, and by the sea the water temperature) is only there if a parent has switched on a weather service. These are **typical values** for the time of year, **not a forecast**. Without a service the list only shows the kind of place and the distance, and says what a service would add. The first time the list fills slowly (see [Why the first time takes a while](/en/settings/services#slow)).
- **Keep as idea** puts a place on your list as an idea. For your own ideas there is **Open**.

The list names no hotel, no price and no connection. Where the numbers come from is stated under the list.

## What WuselWall deliberately does not do {#not}

- Search or book flights, trains and hotels. You search where you always search.
- Watch prices. The day of the price is kept, nothing more.
- Create a calendar appointment for every entry. The plan lives in the trip; the calendar holds one appointment.
- Create tasks for the to-dos or a shopping list for packing. Tasks are chores with points; the packing list has its own items for each person.
- Store PDF tickets or wallet passes, track expenses, read a mailbox, offer a map with pins or a chat.
- Suggest destinations like an assistant: **Where to?** filters a fixed list by your wishes.
- Let children plan. The children's wishes are not part of trips.
