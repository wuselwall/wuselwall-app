## Ready in four steps

<div class="home-steps">
<div class="home-step">

<Shot name="setup-server" alt="The question about the server" />

[1. Install the app](/en/guide/install)

In the browser, on Android or on the iPhone. At first start you enter your family's server, or try the sample family.

</div>
<div class="home-step">

<Shot name="new-family" alt="New family form" />

[2. Create your family](/en/guide/setup#_2-creating-a-new-family)

Sign in and choose the family name and your colour. If you like, start with a few sample chores.

</div>
<div class="home-step">

<Shot name="invite-sheet" alt="Invitation for Oma Ruth with code, QR code and share" />

[3. Invite the adults](/en/settings/members-devices#invite-an-adult)

Your partner, the grandparents or an au pair get a link, a QR code or a code.

</div>
<div class="home-step">

<Shot name="pair-device" alt="New device shows a QR code and a code" />

[4. Connect the tablet and the wall](/en/guide/setup#_4-connecting-a-family-tablet-or-wall-display)

The new device shows a QR code. Your phone scans it, and the device belongs to the family.

</div>
</div>
