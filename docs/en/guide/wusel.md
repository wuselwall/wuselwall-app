# Wusel and colours

WuselWall should not only keep order but also make the day a little lighter. That is what **Wusel** is for, a small round creature that is pleased by what the family gets done, and the **colours**, which follow the time of day and the year.

## Who Wusel is {#who}

*Wusel* is German for a small child who won't sit still. Our Wusel lives in the kitchen. Wusel's tufts are **the colours of your family members**, so Wusel belongs to everyone and to nobody in particular.

Wusel praises what happened, not the person: "All done. That went well!" rather than "You're great!". Wusel never compares anyone, is never sad or disappointed, and **never sends a notification**. When something is not done, Wusel says nothing at all. That stays a conversation between you.

## Where Wusel appears {#where}

- **My day** on the kids' tablet: Wusel waves and greets the child by name, with one useful thing about today: their own birthday, a family tradition ("Today is Pizza Friday!"), the first lesson ("Today you start with maths."), the warm meal, or what the child is looking forward to.
- **The child's own birthday**: the first time **My day** opens that day, Wusel in the party hat fills the whole screen with confetti for two seconds (a tap closes it sooner). Once a day per device, not during the night hours, and only while the **Birthdays** occasion is on.
- **Waiting and no connection**: if **My day** takes longer than two seconds to load, or the tablet has no connection, Wusel thinks: "Just a moment…" or "Hang on, looking for the internet…".
- **The last chore of the day**: Wusel cheers, with confetti and a short sound.
- **A reward requested**: Wusel is proud: "Sent! A grown-up will decide." When a grown-up says yes on another device, the kids' tablet plays the badge sound.
- **A new badge**: Wusel holds up a star and names the badge, for two days on **My day** and under **Points**.
- **Empty pages**: Wusel peeks over the bottom edge, for example on an empty shopping list: "Nothing on the list yet. Tap a tile."
- **On the wall** Wusel sits in the header. In the first half hour after the night ends (usually 6:00 to 6:30) Wusel waves and says "Good morning, everyone!". At night Wusel sleeps, without a connection Wusel is thinking, and on a birthday there is a party hat.
- **On the wall's chore board** Wusel cheers at the top of a person's column as soon as they have done all of today's chores.
- **Today** on the phone: on a birthday, with a card, and on the first day of the school holidays Wusel waves and wishes you a good time off.
- **Getting started done**: when the last item of the **Getting started** card is done, Wusel cheers once: "That's everything set up. Welcome home!"

Wusel never appears where you are entering something (the shopping tiles and the search above them, the event and chore editors), and never next to errors, the PIN, signing in or deleting. What Wusel says is always there as text as well; a tap on the speech bubble hides the line.

## Wusel on the move {#on-the-move}

On the kids' tablet and on the wall, a lively Wusel does not always stay in one place:

- **A chore ticked** (on **My day**): Wusel hops over to the card, takes the points coin and carries it back to the balance.
- **The last chore of the day**: a high jump for joy with confetti as well, and then Wusel cheers back in its usual place.
- **Back on My day** from another area: Wusel hops in from the bottom.
- **A tap**: Wusel giggles, spins its tufts or winks, depending on where the finger lands. Three quick taps in a row give a giggle that shakes all of Wusel. Afterwards Wusel's eyes follow the finger for half a minute.
- **Tilting**: hold the phone or tablet at an angle and Wusel leans to the side and slides a little (in the app, not in the browser).
- **On the wall** Wusel leaves the corner only when someone ticks their last chore of the day on the chore board: Wusel hops over to that column, cheers there for four seconds and hops back. Tapped, Wusel giggles and follows the finger, but stays in the corner.

Wusel only moves when something has just happened, never on its own, and never sits on a button: a tap goes straight through Wusel. At night, without a connection, on **Quiet** and with **Reduce motion**, Wusel stays in its place.

## Wusel's outfit {#outfits}

Everyone can dress Wusel: **Party hat**, **Crown**, **Wizard hat**, **Flower wreath**, **Sunglasses**, **Scarf**, **Headphones** or **Bow**. Every outfit is there from the start and costs nothing; nothing has to be earned. Wusel wears it whenever it is about that person: on their kids' tablet, on their phone, and on the wall once they have chosen themselves.

You choose it under **Settings → Personal** at **Wusel's outfit** (**Advanced** view). On your own birthday Wusel wears the party hat. On the wall, which belongs to everyone, Wusel otherwise wears nothing.

## How lively Wusel is {#how-lively}

In **Settings → Personal** each person sets **Wusel** (in the **Basic** view too):

- **Automatic** (default): lively for children, quiet for adults
- **Lively**: Wusel moves and talks, and on the kids' tablet and the wall Wusel is also [on the move](#on-the-move)
- **Quiet**: Wusel stays still and appears only on empty pages and special days
- **Off**: no Wusel for this person

For the wall and shared tablets, **This device** has **Wusel on this device** (default: lively). It applies while nobody is chosen; after that, the chosen person's own setting wins.

If **Reduce motion** is on for the device, Wusel stays still in its place and there is no confetti.

## Colours {#colours}

- **Every area has its colour**: the bar at the top and the selected tab at the bottom. Today is yellow, Calendar blue, Tasks coral, Meals apricot, Shopping mint, Timetable lilac, Settings stone. Where the bar has room it shows a small picture: a basket for shopping, a broom and stars for tasks, a calendar page, a pot, a school bag and, quietly, two sliders in the settings. On a narrow phone they leave the room to the title. The colours and pictures only help you find your way; the people's colours stay the strongest.
- **On the kids' tablet** cards you can tap look like stickers: a dark edge and a coloured shadow. They sink in when pressed.
- **The sky**: in the wall's header and on **Today** a sky shows the time of day: dawn, bright day with two clouds, dusk (the sun sets in the hour before the night hours), and on the wall at night a dark sky with stars. **Sky in the header** (under **This device**) switches it off.

### Seasons and occasions {#occasions}

The sky also dresses up for special days. Each occasion has its own switch under **Settings → Celebrations & seasons** (**Advanced** view):

| Occasion | What happens | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Seasons** | blossom, sun, leaves or snowflakes in the sky | on |
| **Birthdays** | bunting in the person's colour and a party hat for Wusel | on |
| **School holidays** | a ribbon, "Holidays!" on the first free day and "School again tomorrow" on the last (weekends and public holidays right before and after count too); on **Today** it sits beside the title | on |
| **Advent** | candles in the header, one more on each Advent Sunday | by your region |
| **Easter** | eggs in the hills from two days before to two days after Easter Sunday | by your region |
| **Orthodox Easter** | the same eggs on the Orthodox date | by your region |
| **New Year's Eve** | small fireworks in the night sky on 31 December and 1 January | off |

"By your region" means WuselWall follows the country of your region. In Germany, Austria and Switzerland Advent and Easter are on and Orthodox Easter is off; in countries that celebrate Orthodox Easter it is the other way round. Both Easter dates are calculated anew each year, and a family can switch on both.

## Sounds {#sounds}

WuselWall has four short sounds: a tick, points arriving, everything done, and a badge or reward. Whether they play for you is set by **Sound when a chore is ticked** in **Settings → Personal**. **Sounds on this device** (under **This device**) silences a whole device; **Automatic** means off on the wall and on elsewhere. There is never a sound at night.

Shopping makes no sound: when the last item is ticked or the trip is finished, the phone shows a small burst of confetti and gives a light vibration, once per trip. At night, and with **Sounds on this device** off, it does not vibrate.

## Looking after the wall {#burn-in}

A wall screen shows the same header for months. So that nothing burns into an OLED screen, **Protect the screen from burn-in** (under **This device**, **Advanced** view) moves everything by up to 2 pixels every 10 minutes. **Automatic** means on for the wall and off elsewhere. You won't notice it.
