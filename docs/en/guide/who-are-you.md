# Who are you?

**Shared devices** and the **wall display** are used by several people. So WuselWall asks at the bottom: **Who are you?** Tap your name (your colour) and your actions are yours — the list shows, for example, who added an item.

<Shot name="kid-who" alt="Children's tablet with the family's names at the bottom" wide />

- Without a name nothing can be changed. The device tells you: "Tap your name first."
- Once you have tapped your name, the names make room for the page. At the bottom right you then see **your name with a door**. Tap it when you are **done**: the device belongs to nobody again and asks the next person.
- On the **wall display**, the device goes back to "nobody" after **one minute without a touch**, so the next person doesn't act as you by accident.

## What children see {#what-children-see}

When a **child under 12** taps their name, their **simple view** opens on every shared device: one page without tabs, with only their own things (see [Children's tablet](/en/guide/wall#simple-view)). At the bottom right, **their name with a door** hands the device on when they are done. A child aged 12 or older keeps their tabs on the children's tablet, and on the tablet for everyone the tabs of the adults, without **Calendar** and **Shopping** where those are switched off for children.

While nobody has tapped their name, a **tablet for everyone** shows the tabs a child may see, because a child may be standing in front of it. **Shopping** and **Calendar** are then missing wherever parents have switched them off for children under [Settings → Family](/en/settings/household#the-areas) (**Shopping for children**, **Calendar for children**). As soon as an adult taps their name, everything is back.

<Shot name="tablet-nobody" alt="Tablet for everyone with nobody chosen and shopping switched off for children: five tabs, the row of names at the bottom" wide />

Names are an agreement, not a lock: if a child taps a parent's name, they see that parent's view. Parent actions that matter still ask for the PIN (see the section on the PIN below).

## Settings on the tablet and the wall {#settings-on-shared-devices}

On a **tablet for everyone** the gear for **Settings** only appears once someone has tapped their name. What you find then depends on who you are:

- **Parents** see all settings, as on their own phone. They confirm changes with their PIN (see below).
- **Children and adults who are not parents** see only their **own settings**: **Wusel**, **Wusel's outfit** and the **sound** when a chore is ticked. They need no PIN for this, and there are no other settings for them there.
- On the **wall display** the gear is always there. If nobody is selected, WuselWall first asks **Who are you?** Settings open only once you have tapped your name; **Cancel** does nothing.

When the device goes back to "nobody", for example after a minute without a touch on the wall, the settings close too.

## The parent PIN

Some things are for parents only — adding or blocking devices, for instance. On a **shared device**, tap your name and enter your **6-digit PIN**. For the next **5 minutes** you can do parent actions. The time ends earlier as soon as you tap your name with the door at the bottom right, the wall goes back to "nobody" or you leave the settings.

- The PIN counts for **you** only: whoever taps their name after you gains no rights from it.
- A wrong PIN shows how many tries are left. After a few mistakes you have to wait a moment; after ten, the PIN is locked.
- **PIN locked or forgotten?** Sign in on your **own phone** and set a new one under **Settings → Account & security → Security → Parent PIN**. That unlocks it at once.

On your **personal phone** you don't need the PIN: you are already signed in.
