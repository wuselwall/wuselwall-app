# Who are you?

**Shared devices** and the **wall display** are used by several people. So WuselWall asks at the bottom: **Who are you?** Tap your name (your colour) and your actions are yours — the list shows, for example, who added an item.

<img class="shot wide" src="/screenshots/en/kid-who.png" alt="Kids' tablet with the family's names at the bottom">

- Without a name nothing can be changed. The device tells you: "Tap your name first."
- On the **wall display**, the device goes back to "nobody" after **one minute without a touch**, so the next person doesn't act as you by accident.

## The guardian PIN

Some things are for guardians only — adding or blocking devices, for instance. On a **shared device**, tap your name and enter your **6-digit PIN**. For the next **15 minutes** you can do guardian actions.

- The PIN counts for **you** only: if someone else taps a different name during that time, they gain no rights.
- A wrong PIN shows how many tries are left. After a few mistakes you have to wait a moment; after ten, the PIN is locked.
- **PIN locked or forgotten?** Sign in on your **own phone** and set a new one under **Settings → Security → Guardian PIN**. That unlocks it at once.

On your **personal phone** you don't need the PIN: you are already signed in.
