# Installing the app

WuselWall runs in the browser, on Android and on iPhone and iPad. Each one connects to **your family's server**: that is where your data lives, not with us.

## In the browser — on any device

Your server comes with its own web app. Open its address in the browser, on a laptop as well as on a phone. You get the address from whoever set up your server, or with an invitation. There is nothing to install.

::: tip On the home screen
On iPhone: **Share → Add to Home Screen**. On Android: **⋮ → Add to Home screen**. WuselWall then opens like an app.
:::

Passkeys aren't available in the browser yet; sign in with email and password.

## Android app — tablet and Android phones

The Android app is coming to **Google Play**. Until it is there, WuselWall works in the browser (see above).

## iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app is coming to the **App Store**. Until then WuselWall works in the browser (see above).
