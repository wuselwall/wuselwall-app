# Installing the app

WuselWall comes in three ways. Which one fits depends on the device.

## In the browser — on any device

Open **[wusel.goomba.ch](https://wusel.goomba.ch)** in the browser, on a laptop as well as on a phone. There is nothing to install.

::: tip On the home screen
On iPhone: **Share → Add to Home Screen**. On Android: **⋮ → Add to Home screen**. WuselWall then opens like an app.
:::

Passkeys aren't available in the browser yet; sign in with email and password.

## Android app — tablet and Android phones

1. On the device, open **[wusel.goomba.ch/download/wuselwall.apk](https://wusel.goomba.ch/download/wuselwall.apk)**.
2. The first time, Android asks whether the browser may install **apps from unknown sources**. Allow it for the browser.
3. Tap **Install**.

**Updates:** open the same address again and install. You stay signed in.

## iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app arrives through **TestFlight** once the Apple account is approved. Until then WuselWall works in the browser (see above).
