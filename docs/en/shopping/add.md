# Adding items

An item should be on the list within three seconds — even one-handed, in front of an open fridge. That is what the **tiles** are for.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/en/shopping-phone.png" alt="Shopping list with tiles on top and items by category below">

## Tiles

At the top you see tiles for what you bought **most recently** and **most often**. Nobody maintains this list: it builds itself when you finish a trip.

- **Tap once** to put the item on the list. Its tile turns green.
- **Tap again** to count up: "2×", "3×" … The small **−** in the list takes one away.
- **Press and hold** to add a note for that item, such as "organic", "500 g" or "tin".

## Search and create

Start typing and the tiles shrink to the matching ones. Case, umlauts and accents don't matter: "kaese" finds "Käse", "klopapier" finds "Toilettenpapier".

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/en/shopping-search.png" alt="Search field with “mil” and matching tiles">

- **Enter** adds the first match.
- If WuselWall doesn't know the name yet, an **"Add …"** tile appears. The item goes on the list as free text and becomes a normal tile after your next trip.

## Changing a note or category

**Press and hold** an item in the list to add a note or change its **category**. WuselWall remembers the category for next time.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/en/shopping-edit-item.png" alt="Dialog for editing an item">

## Who put it there?

Next to each item, a small **dot in that person's colour** shows who added it. "Who put the gummy bears on the list?" answers itself.
