# Adding items

An item should be on the list within three seconds — even one-handed, in front of an open fridge. That is what the **tiles** are for.

At home you plan the list: the search field and tiles on top, the items by category below, with no tick boxes. In the shop you tap **Go shopping** at the bottom, which opens the [shopping trip](/en/shopping/store) as a page of its own. WuselWall remembers on this device where you left off.

## Tiles

The tiles are the **catalogue** of what you have bought: the **8 most recent** items first, then the **most often** bought ones, up to 24 in all. Nobody maintains this list: it builds itself when you finish a trip.

Each tile shows a picture of the item: WuselWall recognises common items by their name, such as milk, bread or apples. An item it doesn't know shows its category's symbol.

- **Tap once** to put the item on the list. Its tile turns green.
- **Tap again** to count up: "2×", "3×" … In the list the count is a bold badge after the name. The small **−** takes one away.
- **Press and hold** for a small menu: **Add and edit** puts the item on the list and opens its card (count, note, category); **Pin tile** fixes the tile at the front for good; **Rename tile** fixes a typo WuselWall remembered; **Don't suggest any more** removes the tile (guardians; the history keeps the item, and buying it again brings the tile back).

### Pinning tiles

**Pinned tiles** always come first, most often bought first, and carry a small pin. So the milk stays where it is even if something else was bought last. **Unpin tile** (in the same long-press menu) lets the tile find its place again. Pinning needs a connection.

## Search and create

Start typing and the tiles shrink to the matching ones. Case, umlauts and accents don't matter: "kaese" finds "Käse", "klopapier" finds "Toilettenpapier".

<Shot name="shopping-search" alt="Search field with “mil” and matching tiles" />

- **Enter** adds the first match.
- If WuselWall doesn't know the name yet, an **"Add …"** tile appears. The item goes on the list as free text and becomes a normal tile after your next trip.
- A new item gets a **category chosen automatically from its name** ("chocolate" goes under Snacks, "pasta" under Pantry). If it is wrong, change it on the item card (below).

## The item card

At home, **tap** an item in the list, or **press and hold** it (in the store too), and its card opens from the bottom:

- **Count:** **−** and **+** change the amount. The change applies at once, offline too, and the row behind the card shows it straight away. **−** stops at 1; to take the item away there is **Remove**.
- **Note**, such as "organic", "500 g" or "tin". It is saved with **Done** (or when the card closes).
- **Category:** a row you can swipe sideways, from "Uncategorised" through the categories in the order of the store. A tap saves at once. WuselWall remembers the category for next time.

If WuselWall chose the category itself, a line below says **"Category set automatically from the name"**. Tap another one and it is yours.

## Removing an item

Swipe an item to the left to take it off the list. **Undo** shows at the bottom for a moment; only then is it really gone. Without swiping: open the item card, then **Remove**. Nothing can be removed on the children’s tablet or on the kitchen wall.

## Who put it there?

Next to each item, a small **dot in that person's colour** shows who added it. "Who put the gummy bears on the list?" answers itself.
