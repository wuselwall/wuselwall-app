# Coop shopping list

In Coop's shops, the passabene hand scanners and the passabene app show the shopping list of your Coop account (Supercard ID). WuselWall can copy the open items of a list there, to a Coop list called "WuselWall". In the store you open it on the scanner or in the app, and the phone stays in your pocket.

This exists only in the **iPhone and Android apps**, not in the web app, not on the wall display and not in the kids' view. It is not a Coop service: WuselWall uses the coop.ch website the way a browser does, and a change to coop.ch can stop it working.

## Signing in once

1. Open **Settings → Devices → This device → Coop shopping list**.
2. Tap **Sign in to Coop**.
3. Sign in with your Supercard ID on Coop's own page. WuselWall reads nothing you type there.

The sign-in applies to this device only. If it expires at some point, the sign-in page appears again by itself when you send.

## Sending the list

1. Open the shopping list in plan mode.
2. Tap **Send to Coop** in the bar at the bottom.
3. A line tells you how many items are on the Coop list now, for example "14 items on the Coop list “WuselWall”".
4. In the store, open the "WuselWall" list on the passabene scanner or in the passabene app.

Each send makes the Coop list "WuselWall" equal to the open items of the list on screen: new items are added, items that were bought, removed or changed leave. Whatever you typed into that Coop list by hand stays. WuselWall never touches your other Coop lists. A count above 1 appears as "2× Milk", a note in brackets after it.

## The trip

Whatever you tick on the scanner stays at Coop; WuselWall does not read it back. So finish the trip in WuselWall as usual with **Finish shopping** (see [In the store](/en/shopping/store)).

## When something goes wrong

- If it says only some of the items arrived, send again for the rest.
- If Coop can't be reached or answers in an unexpected way, nothing changes in WuselWall. Try again later.

## Privacy

Each time you send, the names and notes of the open items go to Coop under your Coop account. Your Coop sign-in stays on your phone; your family's server sees none of it and is not involved.

## Disconnecting Coop

**Disconnect Coop** (in the same place in the settings) signs you out on this device, and WuselWall forgets the link. The Coop list itself stays in your Coop account.
