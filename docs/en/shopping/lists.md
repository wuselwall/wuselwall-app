# Several lists

Not everything comes from the same shop. WuselWall therefore has several lists, such as **Supermarket**, **Drugstore** and **Hardware store**. Pick one at the top; the number after the name shows how many items are still open.

<Shot name="shopping-new-list" alt="The “New list” dialog" />

## Creating a list

1. Tap **New list** at the top.
2. Enter a **name** and choose the **layout**: supermarket, drugstore, hardware store or other.
3. Save.

The layout sets the **categories**, in the order you walk through the shop — so in the store the list reads top to bottom with no backtracking. The hardware list won't suggest bananas.

## The default list

A star marks the **default list** (the supermarket at first). It is the only one the **wall display** writes to. You make another list the default under **Edit list**.

## Editing a list

Select the list and tap the **pencil** to the right of the lists (or press and hold its name). There you change the **name** and make it the **default list**. The **Advanced** view adds the **categories**, in the order you walk through the shop: drag a category by its handle to another place, rename it, add a new one or remove it (its items stay on the list without a category). At the bottom is **Delete list**: open items move to the default list; the list's history and tiles are gone. The default list itself cannot be deleted. Deleting is for guardians; renaming and ordering is open to everyone.

<Shot name="shopping-edit-list" alt="Editing a list: name, default list and categories" />

Every list has **its own tiles**: what you buy at the hardware store never shows up at the supermarket.
