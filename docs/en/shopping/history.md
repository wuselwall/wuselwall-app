# History

Every finished trip is kept. Tap the **clock icon** at the bottom left to open the current list's history.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/en/shopping-history.png" alt="History with two finished trips">

- Trips are sorted by **date**, newest first.
- Tap a trip to see **what** was bought, with count and note.
- The history belongs to the list you have selected.

The history is also how WuselWall learns your tiles: what you buy often and recently comes first.
