# In the store

The list is built for shopping: big targets, usable one-handed and with gloves.

## Starting the trip

**Go shopping** at the bottom of the list takes you into the trip with one tap. It fills the whole screen: no bar on top and no navigation at the bottom, so nothing is in the way. The top shows only the list's name with the number of open items ("Supermarket · 5"), then the items by category in the order of the store — no search field and no tiles, every row with a tick box.

<Shot name="shopping-store" alt="The shopping trip as a page of its own, with tick boxes" />

Two buttons sit at the bottom: **Plan mode** on the left and **Add** on the right. **Plan mode** (or your phone's back gesture) takes you back to the normal list. **Finish shopping** sits at the top right next to the list's name, out of your thumb's way: it is the one action per trip.

WuselWall remembers on this device whether you were last planning or shopping. If you closed the phone in the store, the list reopens in the trip next time. This is only for this device; the family sees nothing of it.

## Ticking

- Tap anywhere on a row (at least 56 pixels tall) to tick an item. A count above 1 shows as a bold "2×" badge right after the name, so you can't miss it in the aisle.
- **Press and hold** opens the [item card](/en/shopping/add#the-item-card) with count, note and category.
- The row stays put briefly: ticks are sent and the list reorders 800 milliseconds after
  the last tick. Tap again to undo a waiting tick immediately. Leaving the list sends
  waiting ticks.
- Ticked items don't disappear; they move to the collapsed **"In the trolley"** section at the end. An accidental tick is undone with one tap.

<Shot name="shopping-trolley" alt="The expanded “In the trolley” section" />

## Forgot something?

**Add** at the bottom right opens the search field and tiles in a sheet over the list. You can add several items one after another; the sheet stays open until you swipe it down or go back. To stop typing, tap the **×** in the search field: it clears the field and closes the keyboard.

<Shot name="shopping-store-add" alt="Add sheet in the store with the search field and tiles" />

## Shopping at the same time

Two people in different aisles see each other's ticks **within a second or two** — nothing to reload.

## Finishing the trip

When you're done, tap **Finish shopping** at the top right. The trip then closes and you are back in the list for planning.

- Everything **ticked** is completed and stored in the [history](/en/shopping/history).
- Whatever is **not ticked** stays on the list.
- The items you bought become **tiles** for next time.

The button is only active once at least one item is ticked.

When the last item is ticked or the trip is finished, the phone shows a small burst of confetti and gives a light vibration, once per trip and without a sound (see [Wusel and colours](/en/guide/wusel#sounds)).

## Offline

The list works without signal too: adding, ticking, changing the count and **Finish shopping** carry on as usual. Anything the server has not received yet carries a small cloud icon, and the top of the list says, for example, **"2 changes waiting to be sent"**. As soon as there is a connection again, WuselWall sends everything by itself, in the right order.

On an iPhone this only happens while the app is open: if you closed it in the shop without signal, it sends the next time you open it. Creating a list and changing the default list need a connection.
