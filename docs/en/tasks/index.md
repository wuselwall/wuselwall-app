# Chores

Chores make everyday life predictable without anyone having to keep reminding. The **Tasks** area has five tabs: **Today**, **Approvals**, **Chores**, **Rewards** and **Points**.

<Shot name="tasks-today" alt="Today tab with the family's open chores" wide />

## Today

This is where today's chores are listed, per person.

- Tap a chore to tick it off. With **Undo** you take it back.
- **Open for anyone** marks chores that any person may take. Whoever gets there first has it.
- The **streak** shows on how many days in a row someone kept up, for example "🔥 3". A **streak joker** keeps the streak alive on a missed day, for example a sick day.

On the kitchen wall, a tap on **Open today** opens the same family chore board.

## Approvals

Everything that someone needs to confirm lands here: finished chores that should be checked, and **reward requests**. Tap **Approve** or **Decline**.

## Creating chores

In the **Chores** tab, tap **New chore**:

- **Type**: **Duty** is expected of everyone and earns few points. **Bonus** is optional and earns points.
- **Points**
- **Repeat**: once, daily, on weekdays, weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly
- **Can be done**: only on the due day, or for some days after it
- **Who**: **One person** (fixed), **Taking turns**, **Whoever wants** (open) or **Everyone**
- **Approval by a grown-up**: points come only after a check

A chore you no longer need can be archived. Its open future entries are then removed.

::: tip Start with the starter pack
The **Chores** tab offers a **starter pack**: five chores, three rewards and four badges to begin with. Everything can be changed afterwards.
:::

## Rewards

Each reward costs points. **Stock** limits the number; empty means unlimited.

- **Needs approval first**: when on, a guardian confirms the redemption with the PIN. When off, you redeem it yourself. That only suits small things.
- Anyone who wants a reward taps **Ask for it**.
- The **History** shows what is waiting, redeemed and already handed over. Mark a reward with **Mark as handed over**.

## Points

The **Points** tab shows each person's **balance** and all **movements**: chores done, ticks taken back, rewards redeemed, corrections and badges. A correction is made with **Correct**, and it always needs a reason.

## Why it is built this way

- **Duties earn little.** What everyone is expected to do should not be paid like work. The streak is their reward.
- **No ranking.** WuselWall does not set children against each other.
- **Small rewards come early.** One bonus chore is enough for the cheapest reward.
- **A sick day does not wipe out a streak.** That is what the streak jokers are for.

You set these values under [Household settings](/en/settings/household).
