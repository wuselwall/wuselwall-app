# Meals

The meal plan answers the question "What's for dinner?". The shopping list grows out of it.

<Shot name="meals-week" alt="Meal plan for the week with meals per day" wide />

## Planning the week

The week shows each day with **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner** and **Snack**. Tap a meal or **Add meal** to plan it. Move with **Previous week**, **Next week** and **This week**. If you remove a planned meal, WuselWall offers **Undo** for a moment.

- A meal is a **dish or a note**, such as "Pizza" or "Leftovers", or a **recipe**.
- You also set the **servings**, who **cooks**, a **time** and **guests**.
- To move a meal, long-press it and drop it on another day.
- On the day of a [family tradition](/en/guide/rituals#traditions) with a meal, the day shows a suggestion such as "Pizza" while that meal is free. One tap plans it.

## Wishes {#wishes}

Children wish for a meal on **My day** on their tablet (see [Meal wishes](/en/guide/rituals#meal-wishes)). Open wishes are at the top of the meal plan under **Wishes**, for example "Jack: Pancakes", and as a card on **Today**.

- Tap a wish, pick a day and a meal, and it is planned. Each meal shows what is already planned there. If a meal is planned, the app asks before the wish replaces it; the cook, the time and the servings stay. A recipe wish fills the shopping list like any recipe.
- A planned wish carries a star and "Jack's wish" in the plan and on the wall.
- The **×** removes a wish (**Remove the wish**). Guardians can do that, and so can whoever made the wish.

A wish is never turned down; whatever isn't planned simply waits. Who may wish and how many wishes may be open is set under [Household settings](/en/settings/household#rituals).

## Suggestions

Not sure what to have? Leave the meal's name empty. WuselWall then shows two rows:

- **Family favourites**: dishes cooked in the last 90 days
- **Not had in a while**: dishes not cooked for more than 60 days

A tap fills in the name and the recipe. The basis is your meal plan of the past days.

<Shot name="meals-suggest" alt="Planning a meal, with the rows Family favourites and Not had in a while" />

## Who is eating?

WuselWall works this out automatically. Anyone who is travelling or away at mealtime, and anyone no longer there after the after-school club closes, is not eating. Only set it by hand when that is not right:

- **Automatic** (the usual case)
- **Eating**
- **Not eating**

## Filling the shopping list

**Fill shopping list** previews what the planned meals still need, which meal each item is for and what is already on the list. Tap **Add item**, and the ingredients go onto the [shopping list](/en/shopping/add). Free-text notes have no ingredients; recipes do. An item that comes from the meal plan carries the label **From the meal plan** and shows which meal it is for, for example "For: Lasagne · Wed".

## Recipes

Under **Recipes** you save what you cook often:

- **Ingredients** with amount, unit and note, in the order you like
- **Steps**, one per line
- **Tags**, such as "quick" or "vegetarian"
- a **Source** link, if you found it online

When you plan a meal, WuselWall suggests matching recipes (see [Suggestions](#suggestions)). Tap one to use it; its ingredients then go on the shopping list.

## On the kitchen wall

A tap on the meal area shows the week's meals. A star marks a meal wish. Tap a dot afterwards to change who is eating.

The wall shows one warm meal per day. Which meal that is, and how school days and free days count, is set under [Household settings](/en/settings/household).
