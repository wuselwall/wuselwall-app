# FAQ

## "This device has been blocked" {#blocked}
A guardian blocked the device. Connect it again with [Connect a family tablet or wall display](/en/guide/setup#_4-connecting-a-family-tablet-or-wall-display).

## "That code is wrong or has expired" {#wrong-code}
Codes last 10 minutes and work once. Tap **Connect a family tablet or wall display** on the new device again, and it shows a new code. After several wrong tries you have to wait a few minutes.

## "The invitation code does not work" {#invitation-code}
Invitations are valid for 7 days and work once. Ask the person who invited you for **Share again** or for a new invitation. The link opens the invitation page in the browser; enter the code in the app under **I have an invitation code** ([Signing in and connecting](/en/guide/setup#_3-joining-a-family)).

## "Tap your name first" {#tap-your-name}
On shared devices you have to say who you are before changing anything ([Who are you?](/en/guide/who-are-you)).

## "PIN locked" {#pin-locked}
Ten wrong tries lock the PIN. Set a new one on your own phone ([Security](/en/settings/security#guardian-pin)).

## "No connection to the server" {#no-connection}
Check Wi-Fi or mobile data and tap **Try again**. The shopping list keeps working offline and sends later ([Offline](/en/shopping/store#offline)); there you only see this message if the device has never loaded the list.

## An item is on the list twice {#duplicate-item}
Adding the same name a second time makes WuselWall count it up to **2×** instead of adding a second row. To keep it separate for once, give it a slightly different name.

## Where are my tiles? {#tiles}
Tiles appear when you **finish** a trip. Until then you type names; after the first trip each becomes a tile.

## How do I change the language? {#language}
Under **Settings → Language** you choose German, English, French or Spanish for this device; the phone's reminders follow it too. With **Automatic** WuselWall follows your device's language (other languages show the German version). The choice stays on the device, in the browser for the web app: every phone, tablet and browser in the family can show a different language.
