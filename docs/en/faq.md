# FAQ

## "This device has been blocked"
A guardian blocked the device. Set it up again — with a new [code](/en/guide/setup#_3-set-up-a-device-with-a-code) or by signing in.

## "That code is wrong or has expired"
Codes last 10 minutes and work once. Create a new one in Settings. After several wrong tries you have to wait a few minutes.

## "Tap your name first"
On shared devices you have to say who you are before changing anything ([Who are you?](/en/guide/who-are-you)).

## "PIN locked"
Ten wrong tries lock the PIN. Set a new one on your own phone ([Security](/en/settings/security#guardian-pin)).

## "No connection to the server"
Check Wi-Fi or mobile data and tap **Try again**. Offline use of the shopping list is still being worked on.

## An item is on the list twice
Adding the same name a second time makes WuselWall count it up to **2×** instead of adding a second row. To keep it separate for once, give it a slightly different name.

## Where are my tiles?
Tiles appear when you **finish** a trip. Until then you type names; after the first trip each becomes a tile.

## How do I change the language?
WuselWall follows your device's language (German or English).
