# Reminders

You get reminders on **your own phone**, once you switch them on: under **Settings → Reminders**. They are created on the phone itself. **None of it is sent to a server or to third parties.** That is why they exist only on a family member's personal device, not on a kids' tablet or the wall display.

<Shot name="reminders" alt="Settings → Reminders" />

## What you get

- **Morning summary**: a notification in the morning, for example "Today: 3 appointments, 2 tasks" and "First up at 8:00: swimming". You set the time. Guardians also see rewards that are waiting for approval there.
- **Reminder before appointments**: a notification before an appointment you are part of starts.

You control both with a switch. The settings apply only to you. By default the morning summary is set to 7:00, and the appointment reminder is off.

## Permission

The first time you switch them on, the phone asks for permission to show notifications. If you say no, there are no reminders. You can change the permission in your phone's system settings.

::: tip On the wall display
The wall display has no reminders of its own. Its appointments are shown on the week anyway.
:::
