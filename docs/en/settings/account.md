# Account and family

These steps concern **your account** or the **whole family**. They are at the bottom of the settings on your **personal phone**, and only there. Know what happens first: the steps cannot be undone, except deleting a family within the 14 days described below.

## Password

Under **Settings → Sign-in → Password** you set or change a password. At least 10 characters; a whole sentence is better. The code by email still works too.

## Leave the family

If you no longer want to belong to a family, tap **Leave family** and confirm with **Leave**.

- Your account leaves the family, and your devices sign out.
- The entries you made stay with the family.
- Afterwards you can start a new family or join another one.

This is not possible if you are the **last guardian with an account**. Then invite a second adult first (see [Members and devices](/en/settings/members-devices)), or delete the family.

## Delete the family

Only a guardian can delete a family, on their own phone. Tap **Delete family**, type the **family's name** to confirm, and tap **Delete family**.

- All devices sign out at once. Nobody can get in any more.
- **For 14 days** a guardian can **restore** the family. Sign in on your own phone and tap **Restore**. All data is still there.
- After that all data is **permanently deleted**.
- If you sign in again during this time, WuselWall offers **Restore** (guardians only) or **Start afresh**. Starting afresh means your account leaves the deleted family, and you create a new one or join another.

## Delete your account

To delete your WuselWall account yourself, tap **Delete account**. The sign-in is removed, and you are signed out everywhere.

- Your **profile** stays in the family with everything you have done. Only the access goes.
- If your account still dates from when sign-in with Apple existed and is connected to Apple, access is revoked at Apple.
- For deletion WuselWall asks whether it is really you. If your sign-in is older than 5 minutes, WuselWall sends a **code by email** before it continues.
- The last guardian with an account cannot delete it (see above).

## Good to know

- Deleting the family deletes it for everyone. Only do it when everyone agrees.
- Export your data first if you want to keep it: [Export data](/en/settings/members-devices#export-data).
