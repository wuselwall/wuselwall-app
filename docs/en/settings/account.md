# Account and family

These steps affect **your account** or the **whole family**. Your password, **Leave family** and **Delete account** are under **Settings → Account & security** on your **personal phone**. **Delete family** is in the **Family profile**, in the **Advanced** view. Before confirming, check which data remains and which is permanently deleted.

On narrow screens or with larger text, action buttons sit below their descriptions; scroll down if needed.

## Password

Under **Settings → Account & security → Sign-in → Password** you set or change a password. At least 10 characters; a whole sentence is better. The code by email still works too, if your server sends email; on a server without email, the password is the way in ([Email](/en/server/email)).

If your sign-in is older than 5 minutes, WuselWall asks for a **code by email** before the change, or on a server without email for your current **password**. After the change your **other devices are signed out**; this one stays signed in.

## Leave the family

If you no longer want to belong to a family, tap **Leave family** and confirm with **Leave**.

- Your account leaves the family, and your devices sign out.
- The entries you made stay with the family.
- Afterwards you can start a new family or join another one.

This is not possible if you are the **last parent with an account**. Then invite a second adult first (see [Members and devices](/en/settings/members-devices)), or delete the family.

## Delete the family

Only a parent can delete a family, on their own phone, under **Settings → Family** (**Advanced** view). Tap **Delete family**, type the **family's name**, and tap **Next**. In the second step, type **DELETE** (in capitals) and tap **Delete family**.

- The family is deleted with **all its data immediately and permanently**. There is no waiting period and no way to restore it, not even for a parent.
- All devices sign out at once. The adults' accounts remain: whoever signs in again can start a new family or join another.

## Delete your account

To delete your WuselWall account yourself, tap **Delete account**. The sign-in is removed, and you are signed out everywhere.

- Your **profile** stays in the family with everything you have done. Only the access goes.
- For deletion WuselWall asks whether it is really you. If your sign-in is older than 5 minutes, WuselWall sends a **code by email** before it continues; on a server without email it asks for your **password**.
- The last parent with an account cannot delete it (see above).

## Good to know

- Deleting the family deletes it for everyone. Only do it when everyone agrees.
- Export your data first if you want to keep it: [Export data](/en/settings/members-devices#export-data).
