# Household settings

Not every family does things the same way: how many points a chore earns, whether children may change appointments, or when the kitchen wall switches over may differ for you. You set this under **Settings → Household settings**.

<Shot name="settings-household" alt="Household settings with the areas tasks, calendar, meals and kitchen display" />

The **Basic** view shows only the most important ones: the region, streak jokers, whether rewards need approval, whether children change their own appointments, the warm meal on school days and free days, the question at the table, our week, meal wishes, this device's night hours, Wusel, the sound when a chore is ticked, the morning summary and the reminder before appointments. **Advanced** shows all the others (switch at the top, or tap **Show all settings** at the bottom). Hidden settings still apply.

Every setting has a **default**, which WuselWall suggests. It suits most families. If your value differs, the setting says **Customised**. **Reset to default** brings it back at any time.

## The areas

In the **Advanced** view:

- **Household and members**: the **region** (country, canton or state, municipality) that public and school holidays follow, and whether the kitchen wall shows **full names** or only **initials**. You choose the region when you add the first school; you can change it here later
- **Tasks**: points for a duty and for a bonus chore, whether new chores are duties or bonuses, whether guardians check chores, how many **streak jokers** there are per month and whether **rewards need approval**
- **Calendar and school**: whether children change their own appointments, which new appointments appear on the kitchen wall, when uncertain appointments are asked about, whether children may report timetable changes and whether the **equipment hint** appears
- **Meals**: whether there is a **warm meal** on **school days** and on **free days** (weekends, public holidays, school holidays), that **away means not eating**, and until when care counts as lunch
- **Kitchen display**: when the wall moves on to the coming week and where morning, afternoon and evening split
- **Rituals** and **Seasons and occasions**: see [below](#rituals)
- **This device**: the night theme hours and the settings for Wusel, sounds, the sky and burn-in protection. An opened detail returns to the wall after a minute (see [below](#this-device))
- **My settings**: **Wusel** and **Wusel's outfit**, the sound when a chore is ticked, the morning summary and reminders before appointments. These apply only to the person you chose at the top (see [Wusel and colours](/en/guide/wusel#how-lively)).

Each setting's description is right below it and explains what changes.

## Rituals {#rituals}

The [rituals](/en/guide/rituals) are all on at the start. Each can be changed or switched off on its own:

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Question at the table** | when a conversation question appears: **Warm meal**, **Lunch and dinner** or **Off** | Warm meal |
| **Question at lunch**, **Question at dinner** | the times when the question shows | 11:30–13:30, 17:30–19:30; in France dinner 19:00–21:00, in Spain 13:30–15:30 and 20:00–22:00 |
| **Questions for** | **All ages** or **Older children too** (deeper questions for 8 and over) | All ages |
| **Our week** | weekday and time of the look back at the week, or off | Sunday 16:00 |
| **Meal wishes** | who may wish for a meal each week: **Children**, **Everyone** or **Off** | Children |
| **Open wishes per person** | how many wishes may wait at once (1–3) | 1 |
| **Start looking forward** | how many days ahead a countdown appears (7–60) | 14 days |
| **Count down to a parent's return** | "Back in 2 sleeps" when an adult travels for two nights or more | on |

<Shot name="settings-rituals" alt="Household settings, Advanced view: the Rituals section with the question at the table and its times" />

Below, in **Seasons and occasions**, you switch the decorations in the sky for **Seasons**, **Birthdays**, **School holidays**, **Advent**, **Easter**, **Orthodox Easter** and **New Year's Eve** on or off one by one. What each occasion shows and which ones are on for your region is under [Wusel and colours](/en/guide/wusel#occasions).

## This device {#this-device}

These settings apply only to the device you change them on, for example the wall:

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Wusel on this device** | how lively Wusel is while nobody is chosen: **Lively**, **Quiet** or **Off** | Lively |
| **Sounds on this device** | silences the whole device; there is never a sound at night | Automatic: off on the wall, on elsewhere |
| **Sky in the header** | the sky that changes with the time of day | on |
| **Protect the screen from burn-in** | moves everything by up to 2 pixels every 10 minutes, for OLED screens | Automatic: on for the wall, off elsewhere |

::: tip Try the defaults first
The defaults are a good start. After two weeks of use, have a look whether anything bothers you.
:::
