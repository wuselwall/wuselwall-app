# Members and devices

This belongs to the **guardians**. On your phone, open the cog at the top right (**Settings**).
When you came from another view, swipe inward from the left edge on iPhone or iPad to return; the back arrow at the top left works too.

<Shot name="settings-phone" alt="Settings with members and devices" />

## Basic or Advanced {#view}

At the top of the settings you choose the view. **Basic** is the default and shows what matters most. The settings are sorted into groups (see [All settings](/en/settings/household)). **Basic** shows only the groups with something important in them: **Personal**, **Family** (family profile, region, members, usual week), **Tasks & rewards**, **Calendar & school**, **Meals**, **Rituals**, **Devices** (This device, All devices), **Help & about** and, on your own phone, **Account & security** with **Delete account**. **Advanced** also shows **Celebrations & seasons**, **Kitchen wall**, **Sharing and connections** (sharing the family calendar, outside calendars), the **server**, sign-in, security, and leaving the family.

Whatever is hidden **still applies**. The view only changes what you see, and WuselWall remembers it for each device. The same goes for the group pages, a person's page, the **family profile** and editing a shopping list: Basic shows what most families need, Advanced the rest.

## Invite an adult

Invite a person who should take part in the family with their own account. Open **Settings → Family → Members** and tap **Invite an adult**:

1. Enter the **name** and choose the **role**: **Guardian** sees everything and approves rewards. **Member** does tasks but does not approve rewards (for example an au pair or grandparents).
2. Optionally an **email address**. If the person signs in with exactly this address, they find the invitation by themselves.
3. Tap **Create invitation**.

<Shot name="invite-sheet" alt="Invitation for Oma Ruth with code, QR code and share" />

WuselWall then shows the **code**, your family's **server**, a **QR code** and the **link**. Tap **Share** to send them, for example in a message. The link and the QR code name the server: the person taps the link or scans the QR code, and WuselWall opens directly with the invitation, without choosing a server. Whoever only has the code enters it in WuselWall, together with the server if asked; see [Signing in and connecting](/en/guide/setup#_3-joining-a-family).

An invitation is valid for **7 days** and works **once**. Open invitations appear directly in the invited member’s row with their expiry date: **Share again** sends it once more, **Withdraw** cancels it.

Once the person has joined, **every guardian** gets a notice on the **Today** tab ("Sarah joined"). **Got it** hides it, **Members** leads to the list. Notices disappear on their own after 14 days.

If you left out the email address, the code is all there is. The person then has to enter it in the app.

For an existing **member** without an account, invite them from the member list with **Invite**.

**Invite your partner** on the **Getting started** card first asks **Who would you like to invite?** when the family already has adults without an account, such as the person you added when you created the family. Pick them, and the invitation is for exactly that person, without adding them a second time. **Someone else** adds a new person.

::: tip No account needed
Children do not need an invitation. Add them as a member with a name, and connect their device as described under [Connect a device](#connect-a-device).
:::

## Add member

With the icon at the top of the **Members** page you add a person by **name** only, for example a child or someone who is not on WuselWall. WuselWall gives the next free **colour** automatically.

## Edit a member {#edit-a-member}

Tap a person in the list. On their page you change the **name**, the **colour** and the **picture**: an animal or a symbol instead of the initial, shown on the kids' tablet and in the lists. **Child or adult** decides whether the person gets the kids' tablet, starter chores and the timetable; guardians and people with an account are always adults. A link to the person's **usual week** is there too.

Under **Birthday**, **Choose a date** sets the birthday. It is shown on the wall on the day, counted down to before it, and Wusel wears the party hat (see [Rituals](/en/guide/rituals#birthdays)). **Remove the birthday** deletes it again.

<Shot name="settings-member" alt="A member's page with name, colour, picture and birthday" />

In the **Advanced** view the page also shows the **role** (guardian or member; a child cannot be a guardian, and the last guardian with an account stays one) and **No longer with us**: the person disappears from the wall, the avatar rows and new chores; history and points stay, the birthday is deleted, and you can bring them back here at any time. An adult with an account is not removed this way; they leave the family from their own phone (see [Account and family](/en/settings/account)).

Everyone can change their own name, colour, picture and birthday. Everything else is for guardians, with the PIN on shared devices.

### Changing the order

The order of the members in the list is the order on the wall and in every list. Guardians drag a person by the **handle** on the right to another place.

## Reset two-factor

If another guardian has lost their second factor, tap **Reset two-factor** next to their name. Then they can sign in without the second factor and set it up again (see [Security](/en/settings/security#two-factor-sign-in)).

## Reset password {#reset-password}

Only on a server without email ([Email](/en/server/email#forgotten-password)): if another adult has forgotten their password, tap **Reset password** next to their name and then **Create code**. The app shows an 8-character code that works once, for 10 minutes. Hand it over in person. On their device, they tap **Forgot your password?** when signing in and enter their address, the code and a new password.

## Connect a device

To connect a kids' tablet, a shared tablet or the wall display: under **Settings → Devices → All devices**, tap **Connect a device**, scan the QR code or type in the code the new device shows, and choose the kind and a name. The whole process is shown under [Signing in and connecting](/en/guide/setup#_4-connecting-a-family-tablet-or-wall-display).

<Shot name="pair-approve" alt="A guardian's phone: check the device and connect it" />

You do not need to type the code: the new device shows a QR code that you scan with your phone's camera. It also contains the server; WuselWall opens right at the confirmation. If the link belongs to a different server than the one you are signed in on, the app does not follow it by itself but tells you so.

Older devices with an older version of WuselWall still get a code from **Create a code for an older device**. This option goes away with the next version.

## Rename a device

Tap a device in the list to give it another **name**, for example "Kitchen tablet" instead of "iPad". Under each device you see when it was **last active** ("last active 3 days ago"), so you know which device it is before blocking it. Anyone can rename their own device; other devices are renamed by guardians.

## Block a device

If a phone is lost or a tablet is handed on: in the device list, tap **Block**. The device loses access **at once**, even if it is open. Blocked devices stay in the list so that you can see what happened.

When you sign in with your account on a new phone, your other phones show the notice "New sign-in: Daniel's iPhone" on the **Today** tab. **Devices** leads to the device list, where **Block** shuts out a device that is not yours.

On your own phone you can also sign yourself out with **Sign out this device**.

## Server {#server}

Under **Settings → Server** (**Advanced** view), this shows which server the app talks to, with its **QR code**. A new device scans it at the question **Which server?** and is then connected to the same server.

You only need **Change server** when your family moves to another server. The device signs out and forgets everything stored on it, including shopping it could not send yet. On shared devices WuselWall asks for a guardian's PIN first.

## Family {#family}

Under **Settings → Family → Family profile** you change the **family's name** chosen when it was started. In the **Advanced** view the page also has the **time zone** (the wall and reminders follow it; tap it and search, for example, "Zurich") and the **language on the wall**, in which public holidays are named on the wall display. All of this is for guardians.

## Export data

Guardians can save all data of your family as one zip file, for example for a move to another server. It is under **Settings → Family → Family profile** (**Advanced** view), with **Export data**, and only from a guardian's own phone.

The file contains what you entered: members, appointments, timetables, tasks, recipes, shopping lists and so on. Passwords, PINs and sign-in data are **not** included, and devices appear only with their name and date.

The zip file holds one JSON file per kind of data in the `tables` folder, plus `export.json`, which says what is included and how many entries each file has. After a few years with WuselWall the export can take a minute; keep the app open until it is done. An older server gives you a single JSON file instead.

Leaving and deleting a family is described under [Account and family](/en/settings/account).

## Outside calendars

You subscribe to calendars from clubs or the town under **Settings → Calendar & school → Add outside calendars** (**Advanced** view). How this works and what to note is described under [Calendar](/en/calendar/#outside-calendars).

## Show Getting started again

If you hid the **Getting started** card on the Today tab, bring it back under **Settings → Help & about → Getting started**. The card shows what is still missing: inviting adults, connecting a tablet, setting up the wall display, setting the PIN, subscribing to calendars, entering the timetable and switching on reminders (see [Reminders](/en/settings/reminders)).
