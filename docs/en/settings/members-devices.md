# Members and devices

This belongs to the **guardians**. On your phone, open the gear at the top right (**Settings**).

<Shot name="settings-phone" alt="Settings with members and devices" />

## Adding a member

Tap **Add member** and enter a **name**. WuselWall assigns the next free **colour** automatically. New people are "members"; adding further guardians isn't possible yet.

## Adding a device

1. Tap **Add device**.
2. Enter a **device name**, choose the **type** (shared or wall display) and tap **Create code**.
3. On the new device, enter the code or scan the QR code ([Signing in and connecting](/en/guide/setup#_2-connect-a-device-with-a-code)).

The code is valid for **10 minutes** and works **once**.

## Blocking a device

If a phone is lost or a tablet changes hands: tap **Block** in the device list. The device loses access **immediately** — even if it is open right now. Blocked devices stay in the list so you can see what happened.

On your own phone you can also sign out yourself with **Sign out this device**.
