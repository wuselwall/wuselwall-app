# Calendar

The calendar shows everyone's appointments and, at a glance, who is where. The adults' home screen is **Today**.

<Shot name="calendar-week" alt="Week view of the calendar with presence at the top and appointments below" wide />

## Views

The title, date navigation, view selector and settings share one compact bar. On a phone, the calendar opens in **Day** so appointments are easy to read. Open **Calendar view** to choose **Day**, **3 days**, **Week** or **Month** at any time; on a wide screen it opens in Week. Your choice stays when you switch tabs or rotate the device. The calendar icon returns to **Today**.

<Shot name="calendar-phone" alt="Calendar on a phone with date navigation and the view menu in one bar" />

At the top you choose **Day**, **3 days**, **Week** or **Month**. Move with **Previous**, **Next** and **Today**; on a phone or tablet, a swipe left or right does the same. Two rows stay visible at the top:

- **Presence**: one row per person. It shows who is at work, working from home, travelling, away, off or in childcare.
- **All day**: appointments that last the whole day.

Below them, the appointments follow by time.

Short events keep their labels at the actual times, while taller, easy-to-tap blocks can sit side by side. Template cards adapt to your device’s text size.

## Month {#month}

**Month** shows six weeks at a glance, Monday to Sunday, with the week number on the left. It is for planning, not for editing. Each day shows:

- **public holidays** by name. They come from your household's region (see [Timetable](/en/school/))
- **birthdays** with a cake and the age, such as "Olivia turns 9". Someone born on 29 February appears on 1 March in other years
- **school holidays** as a light band across their days, with their name
- appointments shown **on the kitchen display** and **family traditions**, with title and picture

All other appointments are only counted, for example "+2". When not everything fits, on a phone or with large text, the number also counts what had no room. Presence does not appear in the month.

Today has a frame. **Previous** and **Next** move by a month, **Today** returns to the current month. Tap a day to open it in the **Day** view: there you see every appointment and edit it.

Birthdays always appear in the month, even with the **Birthdays** occasion switched off under **Seasons and occasions** (see [Wusel and colours](/en/guide/wusel#occasions)). That switch only affects the decoration; the month is for looking things up.

## Creating an appointment

Tap **New event**. It starts on the day the calendar is showing (today, while today is in view).

### Starting from a template {#templates}

First you see tiles, so you don't have to type everything from scratch:

- **Templates**: **Cleaner visit**, **Sports practice**, **Doctor’s appointment**, **Guests for dinner**, **Family day out**, **Playdate**, **Cinema trip**, **Family holiday** and **Birthday** (all day, yearly). Each fills in the title, picture, time, length and people. Sports practice starts at 17:00 for one hour, a playdate at 15:00 for two hours with the children, and a cinema trip at 16:00 for two hours with the family. A family day out lasts one day; a family holiday starts with seven. **Cleaner visit** repeats weekly on the chosen day at 09:00 for three hours, for the person who enters the visit. A family with a child also sees **School**: **Class meeting for parents** for a group meeting, **Parent–teacher meeting** for an individual appointment, **School trip**, **School test**, **School fair** and **School sports day**. You can change all these defaults. **Start blank** opens the empty editor at the next full hour.
- **Your templates**: the ones you saved yourselves. In the editor, tap **Save as template** at the top; it keeps the title, picture, time, length, people, location and notes, not the date and not the repeat.
- **Often in your family**: events you have entered at least three times, such as "Grandma & Grandpa visit". WuselWall only counts your own events on your own server; nothing leaves it. Under **⋮** you choose **Keep as template** or **Don't suggest again**.

What a tile fills in is only a start: change what differs in the editor and save. **Back** takes you to the tiles again.

Under **School** there is also **Something to bring** ("For one child, on one day"). It is not an event: you note a one-off thing for one child, such as a gym bag, and it joins that child's packing list (see [Note something to bring once](/en/school/#one-off)).

Next to **Holiday / trip** there is the tile **Plan a trip**: it opens [Trips](/en/trips/#find), where you compare options, look for time windows and make the packing list. **Holiday / trip**, by contrast, only creates a single appointment.

<Shot name="calendar-templates" alt="New event with tiles: Often in your family, Your templates and templates such as Birthday and Guests for dinner" />

### In the editor

You can:

- enter a **Title**, a **Location** and **Notes**. While you type, the title and the location propose what you entered before; a tap takes the proposal over (for a title along with its location and picture, where those are still empty)
- choose between **Appointment** and **Presence**. Presence has six states: At work, Home office, Travelling, Away, Off and Childcare
- make it **All day**, or set **Starts** and **Ends**. Everywhere in the app you set a time with two wheels, one for the hour and one for the minutes in steps of five; **Every minute** shows all of them. With a keyboard you simply type the time, such as "1430"
- choose a **Reminder before** for this appointment right below (see [below](#event-reminder))
- pick people under **Who**. If nobody is picked, the event is for the whole family
- switch **Show on the kitchen display** on or off right below. A line says whether the event will show on the kitchen display and changes as you pick people: on their own, only events for the whole family go there. What new events do by default is set under [Settings → Calendar & school](/en/settings/household)
- tick **Not sure yet** if it is still unclear whether it happens
- choose a **Picture** and mark the event as **We are looking forward to it** or **Family tradition** (see [below](#picture-countdown-tradition))

<Shot name="calendar-editor" alt="Editor for a new event with title, who and repeat" />

### Repeating

Under **Repeat** you choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**. For shift plans there is **Specific days (shift plan)**: you tap the days you work in a month grid. **Repeat ends** sets a date or a number of times.

When you change or delete an event that repeats, WuselWall asks: **Only this event** or **All events in the series**.

### Reminder before {#event-reminder}

Under **Starts** and **Ends** you choose **Reminder before**: **None**, **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **2 hours**, **1 day**, **2 days** or **1 week**. An all-day appointment offers only **1 day**, **2 days** and **1 week**; its reminder comes at 9:00 in the morning, that many days before.

The reminder is saved with the appointment. Everyone the appointment is for gets it: the people under **Who**, or the whole family if nobody is picked. Each person gets it on their own phone, and only where reminders are switched on (under [Settings → Reminders](/en/settings/reminders)) and the phone allows notifications. It is created on the phone itself; nothing is sent to a server or to third parties. That is why a phone learns about a new or changed reminder only the next time the app is opened on it. The text says "Starts at 8:30", or "Tomorrow at 8:30" or "In 2 days at 8:30" when it comes earlier.

For this appointment it replaces your personal setting **Reminder before appointments**: there is no second notification, and it comes even when that switch is off. A repeating appointment has one reminder for all its dates; saving a change with **Only this event** does not change it. **Presence** has no reminder. If you do not see the choice, your family's server is older; it appears after the server is updated.

### Picture, looking forward and tradition {#picture-countdown-tradition}

At the bottom of the editor are three things that make the day lighter (see [Rituals](/en/guide/rituals)):

- **Picture**: one of 37 pictures, such as a cake, the zoo, the dentist, a bike or the cleaner coming. It is shown with the event on **Today**, in the calendar, on the wall and with its countdown. **No picture** is the default.
- **We are looking forward to it**: the event counts down on the wall and on the children's tablet, for example "Trip to the zoo: 3 more sleeps".
- **Family tradition**: something the family does again and again, such as game night. A tradition is always on the wall, with a ribbon. Under **With a meal** you enter the meal that belongs to it and choose **Lunch** or **Dinner**.

## New tradition {#new-tradition}

On a tablet or computer a small button with confetti sits above **New event**: **New tradition**. On a phone, tap **New event** and then the **New tradition** tile. Both open a choice of six starters, **Game night**, **Pizza Friday**, **Film night**, **Sunday breakfast**, **Story time** and **Day out**, and below them **Your own tradition**. Each starter says when it begins, such as "Every Friday, 6:00 PM".

Then the usual editor opens, already filled in: the name, the picture, **Weekly** on the right day, the whole family under **Who** and **Family tradition** switched on. Pizza Friday brings **With a meal** "Pizza" for dinner. Adjust the day, the time and the people, then tap **Save**. With **Your own tradition** you enter the name and picture yourself. More under [Family traditions](/en/guide/rituals#traditions).

## Meetings until …

Tap a day's heading to set **Meetings until**: from that time on you are not available. It shows on the kitchen wall, for example as "Meetings until 17". It shows only the time, no appointments and no details. With **Apply to the whole week (Mon–Fri)** it applies to every weekday of that week.

## Usual week

Under **Settings → Family → Usual week** you record for each day of the week where you normally are, for example in the office on Mondays and working from home on the other days. Tap a day, choose the place and tap **Apply**. **Tuesday to Friday like Monday** makes Monday count for the whole working week. A day with **Nothing set** stays empty on the kitchen wall, as the weekend usually does. Finally, tap **Save**.

If you are not in the same place all day, choose **Time slots**: up to three a day, for example the office from 8 to 12 and working from home from 13 to 17. Today the kitchen wall shows the slot that applies right now, on the other days the first one, with "+1" saying another follows.

If your plan repeats every two weeks, switch on **Alternating weeks (A/B)**. WuselWall asks once whether this week is week A or week B. After that you enter a week of its own under **Week A** and **Week B**. If you switch it off, week B stays saved and is back when you switch it on again.

After that you only enter the exceptions. A normal week stays empty. On a public holiday the kitchen wall shows no usual place.

### Example: one Tuesday off

You normally work in the office on Tuesdays. Set that once in your **usual week**. For a single Tuesday off, create a **Presence** event in the calendar with the state **Off** and **All day** switched on. That event overrides the usual place; the following Tuesday follows the usual week again.

## Children and appointments

You decide under [Settings → Calendar & school](/en/settings/household) whether children may create and change appointments. Deleting someone else's appointment stays with the parents.

## Sending an appointment to another family {#share-event}

You have arranged a games night with another family and want them to have the date. Open the appointment and tap **Share event** at the top. This is for parents, on their own phone or tablet.

WuselWall makes a small **calendar file** and hands it to an app on your device, for example your messenger or your mail. The others open the file and add the appointment to their own calendar: Outlook, the iPhone calendar, Google Calendar or any other.

- **This is sent:** the title, the time and the place. You see it before you send.
- **Include the notes** adds your notes. It is off until you switch it on.
- **Reminder for the recipients** lets their calendar app remind them, an hour or a day before. Some apps ask first or leave it out.
- **Not sent:** who takes part, your family's name and everything else in your calendar.

For a repeating appointment you choose **Only this event** or **All events in the series**.

The file is a copy. If the appointment changes later, share it again; most calendar apps then update their copy. Answers do not come back to WuselWall: the others reply where you sent the file.

## Adding an appointment somebody sent you {#receive-event}

If WuselWall cannot add the file, the page explains why. Choose **Close** to go back; nothing is saved.

Another family sent you a calendar file (it ends in **.ics**)? A parent can add it to the family calendar, on their own phone or tablet.

- **From the message:** tap the file and choose **WuselWall** under "Open with".
- **From the app:** Calendar → **New event** → **From a file**, then choose the file. This is also the way in the browser.

WuselWall shows the appointment first: title, time, place and notes. Choose who takes part (nobody chosen: the whole family) and tap **Add to the calendar**. Nothing is saved before that.

The appointment is then your own copy: you can change or delete it like any other. The sender is not told anything.

If the same appointment is sent again after a change, WuselWall recognises it and offers **Update the appointment** instead of adding it twice. A file with several appointments is not accepted here; a whole calendar belongs under [Outside calendars](#outside-calendars).

If the file belongs to a booking and you have a planned or decided trip, WuselWall also offers **Add to trip**: it then becomes an entry of the trip instead of a calendar appointment (see [A booking's calendar file](/en/trips/#booking-file)).

## Appointments in Outlook or on an iPhone

The family calendar can be shown as a **subscription** in Outlook or on an iPhone, read-only. See [Sharing the family calendar](/en/calendar/feeds).

## Outside calendars

Appointments from outside come into the family calendar through a **subscription**: a club's fixture list, the town's bin collection calendar, school events. You need only the calendar's address, usually a file ending in **.ics**. **webcal://** addresses work too.

A parent manages subscriptions under **Settings → Advanced → Calendar & school → Add outside calendars**. **Subscribe to a calendar** opens the form with name, address, colour, **Who?** (without a choice the calendar is for the whole family) and **Show on the kitchen display**. The appointments appear in the calendar in the calendar's colour, with a subscription mark. They are read-only: changes come from the source.

Your server fetches each calendar about once an hour. **Refresh now** does it at once. If a fetch fails, the reason shows on the calendar, for example "The address cannot be reached right now."

Good to know:

- Calendars are fetched over **https://**; **webcal://** addresses are converted to HTTPS automatically. Addresses that point to machines on your own network are refused.
- A calendar may be at most 2 MB. A fetch gives up after 15 seconds.
- Every device of your household sees only the host name of the address, for example "abfuhr.example.de". The full address is shown only to parents, when they edit the calendar. If it contains a secret key, treat it like a password.
- In the subscription for Outlook and iPhone, outside appointments appear only when you tick **Include outside calendars** when creating it. See [Sharing the family calendar](/en/calendar/feeds).
