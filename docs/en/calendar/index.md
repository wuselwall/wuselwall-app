# Calendar

The calendar shows everyone's appointments and, at a glance, who is where. The adults' home screen is **Today**.

<Shot name="calendar-week" alt="Week view of the calendar with presence at the top and appointments below" wide />

## Views

The title, date navigation, view selector and settings share one compact bar. On a phone, the calendar opens in **Day** so appointments are easy to read. Open **Calendar view** to choose **Day**, **3 days**, **Week** or **Month** at any time; on a wide screen it opens in Week. Your choice stays when you switch tabs or rotate the device. The calendar icon returns to **Today**.

<Shot name="calendar-phone" alt="Calendar on a phone with date navigation and the view menu in one bar" />

At the top you choose **Day**, **3 days**, **Week** or **Month**. Move with **Previous**, **Next** and **Today**. Two rows stay visible at the top:

- **Presence**: one row per person. It shows who is at work, working from home, travelling, away, off or in childcare.
- **All day**: appointments that last the whole day.

Below them, the appointments follow by time.

## Month {#month}

**Month** shows six weeks at a glance, Monday to Sunday, with the week number on the left. It is for planning, not for editing. Each day shows:

- **public holidays** by name. They come from your household's region (see [Timetable](/en/school/))
- **birthdays** with a cake and the age, such as "Olivia turns 9". Someone born on 29 February appears on 1 March in other years
- **school holidays** as a light band across their days, with their name
- appointments shown **on the kitchen display** and **family traditions**, with title and picture

All other appointments are only counted, for example "+2". When not everything fits, on a phone or with large text, the number also counts what had no room. Presence does not appear in the month.

Today has a frame. **Previous** and **Next** move by a month, **Today** returns to the current month. Tap a day to open it in the **Day** view: there you see every appointment and edit it.

Birthdays always appear in the month, even with the **Birthdays** occasion switched off under **Seasons and occasions** (see [Wusel and colours](/en/guide/wusel#occasions)). That switch only affects the decoration; the month is for looking things up.

## Creating an appointment

Tap **New event**. It starts on the day the calendar is showing (today, while today is in view), at the next full hour. You can:

- enter a **Title**, a **Location** and **Notes**
- choose between **Appointment** and **Presence**. Presence has six states: At work, Home office, Travelling, Away, Off and Childcare
- make it **All day**, or set **Starts** and **Ends**
- pick people under **Who**. If nobody is picked, the event is for the whole family
- switch **Show on the kitchen display** on or off right below. A line says whether the event will show on the kitchen display and changes as you pick people: on their own, only events for the whole family go there. What new events do by default is set under [Settings → Calendar & school](/en/settings/household)
- tick **Not sure yet** if it is still unclear whether it happens
- choose a **Picture** and mark the event as **We are looking forward to it** or **Family tradition** (see [below](#picture-countdown-tradition))

<Shot name="calendar-editor" alt="Editor for a new event with title, who and repeat" />

### Repeating

Under **Repeat** you choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks** or **Monthly**. For shift plans there is **Specific days (shift plan)**: you tap the days you work in a month grid. **Repeat ends** sets a date or a number of times.

When you change or delete an event that repeats, WuselWall asks: **Only this event** or **All events in the series**.

### Picture, looking forward and tradition {#picture-countdown-tradition}

At the bottom of the editor are three things that make the day lighter (see [Rituals](/en/guide/rituals)):

- **Picture**: one of 20 pictures, such as a cake, a pizza, the zoo or a tent. It is shown with the event on the wall and with its countdown. **No picture** is the default.
- **We are looking forward to it**: the event counts down on the wall and on the kids' tablet, for example "Trip to the zoo: 3 more sleeps".
- **Family tradition**: something the family does again and again, such as game night. A tradition is always on the wall, with a ribbon. Under **With a meal** you enter the meal that belongs to it and choose **Lunch** or **Dinner**.

<Shot name="calendar-rituals" alt="Bottom of the event editor with picture, We are looking forward to it and a family tradition with a meal" />

## New tradition {#new-tradition}

Above **New event** sits a small button with confetti: **New tradition**. It opens a choice of six starters, **Game night**, **Pizza Friday**, **Film night**, **Sunday breakfast**, **Story time** and **Day out**, and below them **Your own tradition**. Each starter says when it begins, such as "Every Friday, 6:00 PM".

Then the usual editor opens, already filled in: the name, the picture, **Weekly** on the right day, the whole family under **Who** and **Family tradition** switched on. Pizza Friday brings **With a meal** "Pizza" for dinner. Adjust the day, the time and the people, then tap **Save**. With **Your own tradition** you enter the name and picture yourself. More under [Family traditions](/en/guide/rituals#traditions).

## Meetings until …

Tap a day's heading to set **Meetings until**: from that time on you are not available. It shows on the kitchen wall, for example as "Meetings until 17". It shows only the time, no appointments and no details. With **Apply to the whole week (Mon–Fri)** it applies to every weekday of that week.

## Usual week

Under **Settings → Usual week** you record for each day of the week where you normally are, for example in the office on Mondays and working from home on the other days. Tap a day, choose the place and tap **Apply**. **Tuesday to Friday like Monday** makes Monday count for the whole working week. A day with **Nothing set** stays empty on the kitchen wall, as the weekend usually does. Finally, tap **Save**.

If you are not in the same place all day, choose **Time slots**: up to three a day, for example the office from 8 to 12 and working from home from 13 to 17. Today the kitchen wall shows the slot that applies right now, on the other days the first one, with "+1" saying another follows.

If your plan repeats every two weeks, switch on **Alternating weeks (A/B)**. WuselWall asks once whether this week is week A or week B. After that you enter a week of its own under **Week A** and **Week B**. If you switch it off, week B stays saved and is back when you switch it on again.

After that you only enter the exceptions. A normal week stays empty. On a public holiday the kitchen wall shows no usual place.

## Children and appointments

You decide under [Settings → Calendar & school](/en/settings/household) whether children may create and change appointments. Deleting someone else's appointment stays with the guardians.

## Appointments in Outlook or on an iPhone

The family calendar can be shown as a **subscription** in Outlook or on an iPhone, read-only. See [Sharing the family calendar](/en/calendar/feeds).

## Outside calendars

Appointments from outside come into the family calendar through a **subscription**: a club's fixture list, the town's bin collection calendar, school events. You need only the calendar's address, usually a file ending in **.ics**. **webcal://** addresses work too.

A guardian manages subscriptions under **Settings → Advanced → Add outside calendars**. **Subscribe to a calendar** opens the form with name, address, colour, **Who?** (without a choice the calendar is for the whole family) and **Show on the kitchen display**. The appointments appear in the calendar in the calendar's colour, with a subscription mark. They are read-only: changes come from the source.

Your server fetches each calendar about once an hour. **Refresh now** does it at once. If a fetch fails, the reason shows on the calendar, for example "The address cannot be reached right now."

Good to know:

- Only **https://** addresses are accepted. Addresses that point to machines on your own network are refused.
- A calendar may be at most 2 MB. A fetch gives up after 15 seconds.
- Every device of your household sees only the host name of the address, for example "abfuhr.example.de". The full address is shown only to guardians, when they edit the calendar. If it contains a secret key, treat it like a password.
- In the subscription for Outlook and iPhone, outside appointments appear only when you tick **Include outside calendars** when creating it. See [Sharing the family calendar](/en/calendar/feeds).
