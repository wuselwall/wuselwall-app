WuselWall is the family organizer for everything that bustles around your home: a shared calendar, school timetables, chores, a meal plan and a shopping list. On the phone for the parents, as a week board on a kitchen tablet, and in a child-friendly view for the little ones.

Good to know first: WuselWall is not an online service. The app connects to a WuselWall server that your family runs itself – on a NAS, a mini PC, a rented server or in your own Supabase project. We never see your data. You can try the app without a server, with a sample family: tap "Try it without a server" on the first screen.

WEEK BOARD FOR THE KITCHEN
• Seven days at a glance, made for a tablet on the wall
• Who is where, what is coming up, what is for dinner
• Every person has their own colour – distinguishable for colour-blind people too
• Tap a child to see their week with the school timetable

SHARED CALENDAR
• Events for everyone or for single family members
• Recurring events and presence such as shifts or trips
• Subscribe to calendars: clubs, waste collection, school
• As an ICS feed in Outlook, Apple Calendar or Google Calendar

SCHOOL TIMETABLES
• Each child's timetable, with more than one school if needed
• Public and school holidays for Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Liechtenstein, down to the municipality
• Substitutions and cancelled lessons

CHORES WITH POINTS
• Hand out chores, collect points, redeem rewards
• Streaks and badges instead of penalties
• No leaderboard between siblings

MEAL PLAN AND SHOPPING LIST
• What's for dinner this week? Suggestions from what you have cooked before
• One shared shopping list, sorted by aisle
• Works without a connection in the shop and syncs afterwards

ONE DEVICE PER PERSON OR PER ROOM
• Own devices for the adults, one shared tablet for the kitchen
• Children need no account and no email address
• Connect new devices with a QR code, lock each device on its own
• Sign in with a code sent by email, or with a password

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• Everything lives on your own server
• No ads, no tracking, no account with us
• Data export for the whole family
• English and German

OPEN SOURCE
